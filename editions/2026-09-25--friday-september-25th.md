## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

Friday is the day to be outside. Expect mostly sunny skies, a high near 71 and a light north wind, with no rain at all and a mild evening that is still in the mid 60s at 7. A nor'easter passes by this weekend and we only catch its outer fringes, so Saturday turns cloudy and breezy with a high near 68, north gusts up to 25 mph and a chance of showers after 8 PM. Sunday is the cooler, damper day, with a high near 65 and a 40% chance of showers from the morning on, and rain amounts look light. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

Friday gets going early for climbers, with the [Vermont Climbing Festival](https://cragvt.org/festival/) opening its three day run at Farr's Field in Waterbury, clinics at 9 and a bluegrass jam and campfire story hour in the evening. It is also the last day to drop off gear for the [Patagonia Gear Swap](https://www.patagoniaburlington.com/swap) on College Street before the shopping days Saturday and Sunday. At 2 UVM President Marlene Tromp talks about higher education in 2026 at the [EEE lecture series](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/518331) at Faith United Methodist Church in South Burlington. From 5 to 7 the BCA Center opens [Barbershop: The Art of Queer Failure](https://www.burlingtoncityarts.org/exhibition/barbershop-art-queer-failure), where artist Ace Lehner gives haircuts first come, first served, and each person getting a cut commits to an act of queer world making before it grows out. [Historic Burlington-Winooski Bridge Posted on Craigslist, for Free](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/historic-burlington-winooski-bridge-posted-on-craigslist-for-free)

❝

*"We thought we might hear more from people if we tried doing it on something like Craigslist," per Seven Days.*

Bridge No. 150, the 359 foot span over the Winooski River that dates to 1928, is now listed on the Vermont Craigslist free stuff page, because federal rules require VTrans to advertise historic bridges for free before tearing them down, and this one is due to be replaced starting next year. The catch is that the new owner has to move it, in pieces since it is too big to haul whole, and build new abutments and piers wherever it lands. The listing stays up for 30 days, and so far the only nibble was one message asking, "Can I move it tomorrow?"

[Solar-powered backpacks are helping Vermont scientists track monarch butterflies in real time](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-09-24/solar-powered-backpacks-helping-scientists-track-monarch-butterflies)

❝

*"It really sort of blows your mind how much these individuals need to go through to make it all the way to Mexico," per Vermont Public.*

Scientists at the Vermont Center for Ecostudies in White River Junction raise monarchs and glue tiny solar powered transmitters to them, each weighing less than a tenth of the butterfly's body weight, that report their location over Bluetooth. The first trackers went out last fall and have already shown that some eastern monarchs winter in Florida and the Caribbean instead of Mexico, and that they spend a surprising amount of time in cities and old industrial sites. You can follow Vermont's butterflies, with names like Ann, Scott and Pam, in real time on the Project Monarch app.

['Justice has been rendered': Jury finds Burlington man guilty in shooting of 3 Palestinian students](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/21/jury-finds-burlington-man-guilty-in-shooting-of-three-palestinian-students)

❝

*"After three years of sorrow, loss and struggle, our families finally feel relief," per VTDigger.*

A jury in Chittenden Superior Court found Jason Eaton, 51, guilty Monday on three counts of attempted second degree murder for shooting Hisham Awartani, Kinnan Abdalhamid and Tahseen Aliahmad near his North Prospect Street apartment on Nov. 25, 2023, after a little less than two hours of deliberation. His lawyers never denied the shooting and argued he was legally insane, pointing to a recording in which he said he heard voices through his FM radio, while the prosecution's expert testified he was acting out after drinking and smoking marijuana under the stress of a job loss. Awartani remains paralyzed, and his mother, speaking for the families, said she believes the men were targeted because they were Palestinian, though no hate crime charge was brought. No sentencing date is set, and Eaton faces up to life without parole on each count.

[More than 3,000 could strike at UVMMC if vote approved](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/more-than-3000-could-strike-at-uvmmc-if-vote-approved/73874550)

❝

*"People are fed up. They're tired of being ignored and are ready to make their voices heard," per NBC5.*

Union members at UVM Medical Center voted Thursday on authorizing a strike, after 18 bargaining sessions and a best and final offer from the hospital Wednesday that the union says includes multiple takebacks from their original contract. Their central demand is minimum staffing levels in every department, and their contracts expired last month. The hospital says it shares the same priorities and wants agreements that live within its financial realities, and the union will know whether the authorization passed next Wednesday at midnight.

[Attorney General Under Fire for Charging ICE Protesters](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/immigration-news/attorney-general-under-fire-for-charging-ice-protesters)

❝

*"Our courts are already backlogged with real threats to public safety, and this is not what our top prosecutor should be prioritizing," per Seven Days.*

Attorney General Charity Clark is prosecuting 13 people arrested at a July 24 sit-in outside the Williston office park where ICE runs a surveillance center, the first charges since protests there picked up last fall, after State's Attorney Sarah George twice declined to charge demonstrators arrested there. Twelve pleaded not guilty Thursday to misdemeanor unlawful protest, Treasurer Mike Pieciak and Senate Majority Leader Kesha Ram Hinsdale want the charges dropped, and Gov. Phil Scott said he applauds the decision. Clark says her personal feelings about ICE cannot stand in the way of the law, a coalition of activist groups has a week of action planned in Williston starting Oct. 4, and the protesters are due back in court in December.

[Burlington updates community on housing strategy as city works toward 7,000 new homes by 2050](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/burlington-housing-strategy/73857411)

❝

*"The city can't build 7,000 homes by itself. We're going to need large projects and small ones," per NBC5.*

Hitting 7,000 new homes by 2050 means about 280 a year, and over the last decade Burlington has averaged 145. About 1,100 homes are now in the development pipeline, more than 250 of them expected to be affordable, and the Mulvaney-Stanak administration wants to double the affordable units required under the city's inclusionary zoning rules over the next decade, with proposed changes coming later this year.

[Poll shows Gov. Phil Scott trailing Democrat Amanda Janoo](https://www.wcax.com/2026/09/24/poll-shows-gov-phil-scott-trailing-democrat-amanda-janoo)

❝

*"It's a fluid race certainly. Things can change here. But if I'm Phil Scott, I should be worried," per WCAX.*

The UNH Survey Center's Green Mountain State Poll has Janoo at 49% and Scott at 43%, a real departure for a five term incumbent who has won by wide margins on the strength of Democratic support, and his approval rating has slid from 67% at the end of the pandemic to 47%. Former Lt. Gov. Molly Gray leads incumbent Republican John Rodgers 54% to 40%, and Becca Balint leads Gerald Malloy 66% to 30% for the U.S. House. Scott's campaign says the sample has too many Democrats in it, and the survey of 835 people ran Sept. 17 to 21 with a 3.3 point margin of error.

[Cold September nights have Vermont's foliage heating up](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/24/cold-september-nights-have-vermonts-foliage-heating-up)

❝

*"With a decent amount of sunshine next week after Monday, expect near peak colors across the Green Mountains by next weekend!" per VTDigger.*

Cold nights have the high elevations already bright, with moderate color across the Green Mountains and the Northeast Kingdom, and the best leaves right now on the high peaks in southern and central Vermont and up in Essex County. Lower elevations are just starting to turn, and moderate color should break out across them by Tuesday everywhere except the Champlain and Connecticut River valleys.

[Burlington Orders Euthanasia for Former Prosecutor's Problem Dog](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-orders-euthanasia-for-former-prosecutors-problem-dog)

❝

*"Moose's long-standing pattern of aggression and reactivity presents an ongoing public safety risk that will not be abated until he is humanely euthanized," per Seven Days.*

Burlington's Animal Control Committee ordered Moose put down Monday after a long record of attacks, most recently in April when he lunged at defense lawyer Betsy Hibbitts outside the St. Albans courthouse and tore her upper lip. His owner, former Franklin County deputy state's attorney Diane Wheeler, resigned after 30 years and told the panel it had no jurisdiction because she had moved out of Burlington, but the committee found she and the dog were seen in the city as recently as this month. In 2025 the committee had ordered her to hand custody to her sister and keep Moose out of the New North End, and she later claimed neighbors were seeing "Moose-like statues."

[For laid off St Albans Creamery workers, health insurance benefits run out next week](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/23/for-laid-off-st-albans-creamery-workers-health-insurance-benefits-run-out-next-week-the-union-representing-them-is-asking-the-courts-to-intervene)

❝

*"Without action from the court, the union's insurance is set to expire at the end of the month," per VTDigger.*

Teamsters Local 597 asked the Vermont District Court on Tuesday to make Dairy Farmers of America keep paying health insurance for workers laid off when the St. Albans Creamery closed Aug. 17, while the union's claim that the closure was illegal retaliation for last year's strike waits on arbitration. DFA covered 85% of premiums and calls the filing without merit, and the two sides cannot agree on an arbitration date before early next year.

[CVU takes win at first football game played on Burlington High's new field](https://www.charlottenewsvt.org/2026/09/24/cvu-takes-win-at-first-football-game-played-on-burlington-highs-new-field)

❝

*"It's Christmas in September, says Champlain Valley Union High assistant coach Rahn Fleming, who's always dreaming of the gridiron time of year," per The Charlotte News.*

Burlington High christened its new football field last Friday after four years of classes in the old downtown mall, and CVU spoiled the party with a 20-6 win over the SeaWolves, the combined Burlington and South Burlington team. Junior Luca D'Agostino, starting at quarterback while senior D'Marcus Riggs recovers from a separated shoulder, ran for one touchdown and threw for another, and Alex Jovell added an interception and two field goals. The game stopped for at least 10 minutes while the parking lot was searched for someone shining a laser pointer onto the field, and CVU hosts undefeated St. Johnsbury Academy Saturday at 1.

[Vermont Conversation: 'There was some justice.' Hisham Awartani on enduring and speaking out for Palestinian lives](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/23/vermont-conversation-there-was-some-justice-hisham-awartani-on-enduring-and-speaking-out-for-palestinian-lives)

❝

*"There was some justice dealt, but obviously it doesn't change what happened," per VTDigger.*

Awartani, now 23 and paralyzed from the chest down, told David Goodman he is grateful for his care at UVM Medical Center and Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital and for the support he got in Vermont, and that the attention to his case felt dissonant next to the death and destruction in Palestine. He graduated with honors from Brown in May with a degree in archaeology and math, and plans to spend a year back in the West Bank before graduate school.
