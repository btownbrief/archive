## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

If you have one outdoor thing to get done this weekend, do it today. Friday is wall to wall sunshine with a high near 79, bone dry air, and not a drop of rain in the forecast, with the lake flat and sitting around 73 degrees. Saturday stays dry and warm near 80, though clouds fill in through the afternoon, so the morning is the brighter half. Sunday opens dry and partly sunny, then showers and thunderstorms are likely from early afternoon on with a humid high near 80, so plan the Sunday outdoor stuff for before lunch. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

## BTown Arts Podcast: Kwame Sits Down with Grammy Nominee HAYLA

**Our arts and entertainment podcast landed a big one!** Kwame Dankwa, who hosts the BTown Brief Arts Podcast, [sat down with HAYLA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W6LBJ72UKvo) at the Ilesoniq festival in Montreal, and if you have been anywhere near dance music lately, you absolutely know the voice. With 6 MILLION **monthly** listeners on Spotify, she is the Grammy nominated British singer behind "Escape" with Kx5, the deadmau5 and Kaskade project, with hits alongside John Summit and collaborations with Kygo, Meduza, Sub Focus and Wilkinson.

The conversation goes way beyond just the resume. HAYLA tells Kwame she was an inherently shy performer who found it hard to sing in front of people, and she walks through the long road before the big stages, waitressing, coaching gymnastics, working security, and three years as a cruise ship singer on the Baltic. Her dad ran his own electrician business and told her he could never work for anybody else, and watching him bet on himself is what convinced her she could too. Her mom's reaction when she committed to music was "I'd be annoyed at you if you decided to do anything else." She also opens up about her brand new album DARK, the follow up to her debut DUSK, written through love, loss and grief, a record she calls a piece of therapy she did not know she needed, and says she plans to take on much more of the production herself on whatever comes next. Check out [DARK](https://open.spotify.com/album/7kpzdqlPXqyEFWVIONkhN4?si=iQbeEjXQSUmo9S_mvFYjxg), from July 31st, on Spotify.

Big, big shout-out to Kwame, he puts real work into these conversations and it really shows in this one. If the voice sounds familiar it should, Kwame is an award winning radio veteran with more than 20 years on the air, and these days he brings that experience to music journalism and to DJing events and festivals all over the area with a heavy rotation of house, Afrobeats and pop. I just touched the surface, so definitely watch the [full 8-minute interview on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W6LBJ72UKvo), catch his [festival wrap-up montage](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcMLPW8SsrD/) from Ilesoniq on Instagram, and give the podcast a follow too. You can find HAYLA at [@haylasings](https://www.instagram.com/haylasings/) everywhere. Check out past interviews here: [BTown Arts](https://open.spotify.com/show/6ejf0OFAyNTZNKDzFLWbKp?si=311d38954ddc49b3). Follow [Kwame on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thekwamevibe/) and book him for your next event!

[Burlington prepares to host first-ever Navy Week](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/19/burlington-prepares-host-first-ever-navy-week/)

❝

*"Some of the actions carried out on the water were pivotal to the birth of our nation and the defense of our nation," per WCAX.*

Burlington Navy Week runs Monday through Sunday, the first Navy Week ever held in Vermont, and most of it is free and on the waterfront. A 34 foot Sea Ark harbor patrol boat does demonstrations off the Community Boathouse Monday and next Friday at 11:30, sailors from the namesake USS Montpelier and USNS Burlington are on display between ECHO and the Boathouse from noon to 5:30 Monday through Thursday, the Ceremonial Guard drill team performs Tuesday at 3:15 followed by a Jack Tar Brass concert, and the brass band plays free shows at the BCA Center Wednesday at 6, Church Street Thursday at 6 and again Saturday the 29th from 2 to 4. The week wraps at the Champlain Valley Fair's opening weekend. Full schedule at [outreach.navy.mil](https://outreach.navy.mil/Navy-Weeks/Burlington-2026/), and Lt. Cmdr. John Stevens joins WCAX's "You Can Quote Me" this Sunday at 11:30 with an update on the USS Vermont.

[Vermont's Miss Black USA reflects on historic win](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/18/vermonts-miss-black-usa-reflects-historic-win/)

❝

*"Often, folks, when they think of Vermont, they're not thinking about Black people. And I just hope that with this newfound title that I have, that, you know, that changes things," per WCAX.*

Harmony Edosomwan, who owns Harmony's Kitchen in Winooski and cooks traditional Black Southern food there, took home the national crown at this year's Miss Black USA pageant. Along with the title she won a full ride to law school, and she says she wants to use the platform to bring more attention to people of color living in Vermont.

[Vermont State Police patrols end in Burlington following violent weekend](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/vsp-burlington-patrols-end/73478291)

❝

*"There's still an issue, obviously, in Burlington, and I hope they work through that," per NBC5.*

The state police pulled their extra downtown patrols Sunday after three weeks, an assignment Gov. Phil Scott ordered ahead of the New England governors and Eastern Canadian premiers conference. The weekend they left brought a stabbing near Bank and St. Paul, the fatal stabbing behind Courthouse Plaza, an exchange of gunfire on lower Church Street and a separate accidental discharge. Scott says there are no plans to bring the patrols back right now but that could change if the city asks. Public Safety Commissioner Jennifer Morrison cautioned against reading the weekend as a trend, pointing to an overall drop in violent crime in Burlington and statewide, and the mayor cited preliminary city data showing violent incidents down from both 2025 and 2024.

[Suspect surrenders and faces second-degree murder charge in downtown Burlington stabbing](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/18/suspect-surrenders-and-faces-second-degree-murder-charge-in-downtown-burlington-stabbing/)

❝

*"There are several witnesses to the fight, and the stabbing was caught on video, as was Mr. Haji's movements after the event," per VTDigger.*

Hassan O. Haji, 24, of Winooski, walked into Burlington police headquarters Tuesday morning and pleaded not guilty that afternoon to second degree murder in the Saturday stabbing behind the Main Street courthouse we noted Monday. Police allege that during a fight around 4 a.m. involving a third man, who has not been charged, Haji stabbed 24 year old Deng Dau once in the groin, and Dau died about an hour later at UVM Medical Center. [Seven Days reports](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-police-arrest-man-for-fatal-downtown-stabbing/) that detectives tracked Haji from the scene using security camera footage and blood evidence, and [WCAX notes](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/18/man-charged-burlington-stabbing-death/) that everyone involved knew each other, with the two families acquainted for years, though police have not said what the argument was about. Judge Robert Katims ordered Haji held without bail, with a review hearing set for August 27, and the charge carries up to life in prison.

I want to add a personal note here, because this one hits close to home for me. Deng graduated from CVU, where he played on the basketball team, and I knew him a bit from playing pickup basketball around the city over the years; a competitive player with the skills and size to back it up. May he rest in peace. The man charged is the brother of someone else I play ball with, so this sits heavy from every direction. Two guys who knew each other, both 24, and an argument ended a young life. My heart goes out to both families and everyone who loved Deng. His family is raising money for funeral costs through a [GoFundMe in his memory](https://www.gofundme.com/f/in-loving-memory-of-deng-dau), which remembers him for his incredible sense of humor and kind heart, and it has raised over $19,000 of its $28,000 goal at last check. If you are able to chip in or share it, please do.

**[Burlington Business Association Shutters After Nearly 50 Years](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-business-association-shutters-after-nearly-50-years/)**

❝

*"We believe in Burlington. We believe in its downtown, its people, and its future," per Seven Days.*

The Burlington Business Association, which started in 1978 as the Downtown Burlington Development Association and helped turn Church Street into a pedestrian mall, announced August 10 in an unsigned email that it is closing after nearly half a century. It had run without a full time director since longtime leader Kelly Devine stepped down last year, and its dues paying members backed the waterfront bike path, City Hall Park and the Champlain Parkway along the way. The other half of this story is the Lake Champlain Chamber's new Downtown Business Alliance we mentioned Monday, which WCAX reports is [being built to give businesses a voice at City Hall](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/17/new-alliance-aims-give-burlington-businesses-voice-city-hall/) as downtown foot traffic and sales slide, and Building Burlington's Future has already picked up the association's downtown ambassador and action group programs.

**[Ignat Solzhenitsyn at Lake Champlain Chamber Music Festival](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/arts-culture/ignat-solzhenitsyn-at-lake-champlain-chamber-music-festival/)**

❝

*"Mobile phones go off with distressing frequency in both houses. But these days the Bolshoi audience is better dressed," per Seven Days.*

Conductor and pianist Ignat Solzhenitsyn, son of the Nobel laureate, grew up in Cavendish from age 4 to 14 after his family's exile landed them in Vermont in 1976, and this is the 50th anniversary of that arrival. He opens the Lake Champlain Chamber Music Festival Saturday at 2 at Elley-Long in Colchester with violinist Soovin Kim and cellist Paul Watkins on a program of Russian composers, then sits down with Kim to talk about the family's journey, and Sunday at 3 he joins "From the Motherland," a tribute to Stravinsky, Rachmaninoff and Shostakovich. The festival's theme this year is "Migrations," and it runs through August 30 at Elley-Long and Meach Cove Farms in Shelburne, with the two Solzhenitsyn concerts at $10 to $49.

**[Payouts soar as use of force by Burlington police on Black residents hits record high](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/17/payouts-soar-as-use-of-force-by-burlington-police-on-black-residents-hits-record-high/)**

❝

*"The fact that the data show record-high disparities in use-of-force on Black subjects demands our immediate attention and a meaningful response," per VTDigger.*

Since 2019, Burlington has paid nearly $1.5 million, plus $747,500 in legal fees, to settle five police use-of-force cases, all involving Black individuals or people facing mental health challenges. In 2025, force against Black residents hit a record 99 incidents, accounting for 28% of cases despite Black residents making up just 4.7% of the city’s population. While the mayor’s office plans a deeper review and Chief Burke links overall increases to rising gunfire, racial justice advocate Rev. Mark Hughes questions whether further examination will yield actual reform.

Sen.
