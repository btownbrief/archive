## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

If the air feels like soup again today, you are not imagining it. Friday runs hot and humid with highs in the upper 80s, near 90 in the valleys, mostly dry through the afternoon before showers and a few rumbles move in after dark. Saturday wakes up to scattered morning showers, clears into a midday lull, then pops up a few more showers and storms in the afternoon with highs in the mid 80s. Sunday is the best of the weekend, a lingering morning shower chance giving way to a mostly sunny afternoon in the low to mid 80s as the humidity finally breaks by evening.

The [Sidewalk Sale](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/sidewalk-sale-45096015) has the bricks through Sunday, with Church Street merchants hauling their wares outside for a weekend of discounts. South Burlington gets a brand new [farmers market](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/south-burlington-farmers-market/7473/) tonight from 5 to 7 along the airport rec path on Airport Drive, and carnivores can size up the [truckload meat sale](https://www.facebook.com/events/1037645752191712/) running today at NAPA Auto Parts on Williston Road, where the headline deal is 20 ribeyes for $40. Author and illustrator [Selina Alko](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/selina-alko-45317226) reads from her new Judy Blume picture book at Phoenix Books at 5:30, free with preregistration, and walkers can join a [3 mile Rock Point nature walk [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/outdoors-for-everyone-vt/events/315966660/) from Leddy Park at 6. Film people get the [Mary Oliver documentary](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/mary-oliver%3a-saved-by-the-beauty-of-the-world/7752/) at the Film House at 4, with a second screening at 7. The evening splits between [Strapped-In, a queer comedy showcase](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/strapped-in-a-queer-comedy-showcase-45383183) at Vermont Comedy Club at 7, [90 PROOF](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/90-proof-45383037) playing free at The Old Post at 7, and [Half Dead](https://skinnypancake.com/event/dancing-in-the-streets-half-dead/) covering the Dead for free on the Skinny Pancake waterfront patio at 8. If you want something more restorative, [The Good Buzz Island Edition](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/500067) runs sauna rounds, lake dips and a sunset reggae vinyl set at Savu Lakeside from 7 to 10, and two time Grammy winner [Mads Tolling](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/mads-tolling-in-the-loop-tickets-1991785044686) loops his violin into a full band at the Shelburne Farms Coach Barn at 7.

Saturday is stacked. Our own [BTown Coffee Club [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/315748075/) holds down Zero Gravity on Pine Street from 10, free as always, come say hi. Families have options all day, with a [Family Bike Ride to Oakledge](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/508690) rolling from the Local Motion Trailside Center at 10, the [Princess Cruise](https://www.facebook.com/events/4447297015489312/) sailing the Spirit of Ethan Allen at noon with fairy tale characters aboard, and [Field Day & Kites in the Park](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/504482) filling Veterans Memorial Park in South Burlington from 1 to 3 with kite making and sack races. The [Corn Roast & Car Show](https://www.facebook.com/events/1305702125026018/) parks the Champlain Valley Street Rodders at Sam Mazza's in Colchester from 10 to 4 with 1950s rock and roll on the speakers. Shelburne has the big one, the [VTB & VFC Birthday Bash](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/vtb-%26-vfc-birthday-bash-at-vermont-teddy-bear!/7750/) at the Vermont Teddy Bear factory, a free daytime party from 11 to 3 with mini golf and a bouncy castle, then a ticketed evening ($15-$20) from 5 to 9 with DJ Fattie B and a sunset drone light show. The evening lineup runs deep, with teen soul standouts MINC playing their [Summertime Bash](https://www.minctheband.com/summertime-bash) at the SEABA Center at 7 on a $15-$30 sliding scale, the [Vermont Comedy Awards](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/vermont-comedy-awards-2026-45383184) handing out hardware free at Vermont Comedy Club at 6, the documentary [Frequency](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/frequency-the-secret-of-everything-45319995) premiering at Main Street Landing with doors at 6:30, mentalist Dr. Steve Taubman's [Mind Magic ($35)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/mind-magic-a-night-of-laughter-and-astonishment-45270124) at the Off Center at 7:30, dance theater piece [Shadowlight](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/shadowlight-tickets-1981461349235) at the McCarthy Arts Center in Colchester at 7, and [Bop To The Top](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/bop-to-the-top-45382897) closing the night at Higher Ground at 9.

Sunday leads with the [Lake Champlain Dragon Boat Festival](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/lake-champlain-dragon-boat-festival-45364361), paddlers filling Waterfront Park from 8 to 4 in support of Dragonheart Vermont's programs for breast cancer survivors. The [Vermont Brass Trio](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/sunday-classical%3a-vermont-brass-trio/7417/) plays free among the galleries at the BCA Center at 10, and master naturalist Gene O. Desideraggio leads a [Tree ID and Natural History Tour](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/tree-id-and-natural-history-tour-45364750) of Ethan Allen Park at 10, preregistration required. Book people can catch [Helen Whybrow](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/helen-whybrow-45350692) celebrating the paperback of The Salt Stones at Phoenix Books from 1 to 3, free with preregistration. And with the humidity breaking, ska veterans [Save Ferris](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/save-ferris-vallory-falls-45382898) close the weekend at Higher Ground at 8 with Vallory Falls opening.

## Local News (All Links Clickable)

[Church Street Marketplace kicks off 46th Annual Sidewalk Sale](https://vermontbiz.com/news/2026/august/05/church-street-marketplace-kicks-46th-annual-sidewalk-sale)

❝

*"For 46 years, this event has brought people together on Church Street, and that's what makes it such an important tradition," per Vermont Business Magazine.*

The sale runs Wednesday through Sunday with deals spread across the whole Marketplace, from 50 to 90 percent off under the 4T2D tent to Gaku Ramen throwing in free mochi with a meal. Outdoor Gear Exchange brings back its Consignment Yard Sale today through Sunday alongside a new rewards program. If you only browse the bricks once a year, this is the weekend the stores meet you halfway.

[A Burlington Artist Captures the Good News in Town](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/a-burlington-artist-captures-the-good-news-in-town/)

❝

*"It's nice to hear something good once a week. There is so much darkness out there," per Seven Days.*

For the past month, artist Paige Hurley has been turning everyday acts of Burlington kindness into quick color sketches, posting each one on the bulletin board at Speeder & Earl's on Pine Street every Thursday before handing the original to the people in it. Her latest showed two geese flying through a rainbow for a pair of roommates who discovered their grandmothers were best friends in Québec 60 years ago. She planned eight weeks of this and now has enough stories to keep going, and she is still hunting for the stranger on a scooter who handed out chocolates on Charlotte Street, reachable on Instagram if you know them.

[The Best of Vermont: Locals Pick the 2026 Seven Daysies](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/vermont/BestOf)

❝

*"This year's Daysies applaud 230 winners, all of whom locals say live up to the hype," per Seven Days.*

Nearly 14,000 readers cast more than 300,000 votes in this year's Hollywood themed Daysies, crowning 230 winners and 908 finalists across food, drink, culture, shopping and recreation. Burlington did fine for itself, with Honey Road sharing best restaurant honors, La Di Da taking best new restaurant, and Sneakers in Winooski splitting best brunch. Worth a scroll before your next night out!

[Drawing attention: Vermont gets its first tourism branding](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/04/drawing-attention-vermont-gets-its-first-tourism-branding/)

❝

*"We can't just expect tourism to happen to us. We need to continue to invest in it," per VTDigger.*

The state unveiled a hand drawn Vermont wordmark and the motto State of Inspiration, its first dedicated tourism brand, with the letter m shaped like Camel's Hump and taglines like "Farm to table. We just call it dinner." The $275,000 project answers a study that found 79 percent of American travelers rate Vermont appealing while only 15 percent name it unprompted as somewhere they plan to visit. With visitor spending at 9 percent of state GDP, triple the national average, closing that gap is real money.

[Vermont Comedy Arts to Open New Performance Space](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/arts-culture/vermont-comedy-arts-to-open-new-performance-space/)

❝

*"I'm excited to be able to do kids' programming in a space without a bar," per Seven Days.*

The nonprofit arm of Vermont Comedy Club is opening a 30 to 40 seat theater at 118 Main Street, across from the club in the basement space that used to hold the Drink bar. Executive director Natalie Miller wants it ready before next month's Big Pond Improv Festival, giving local standup, sketch and improv performers stage time the 140 seat club can only spare on weeknights. Skipping the liquor license is deliberate, keeping the room open to young performers.

[Rental e-bikes return to Burlington years after Bird Bike fiasco](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/rental-e-bikes-burlington-bird-bikes/73370424)

❝

*"We're paying the price for bird's bad behavior over the last couple of years," per NBC5.*

Burlington Segway owner Rick Sharp has put new rental e-bike fleets on the streets, and unlike the Bird bikes that ended up strewn across sidewalks, these only unlock and return at designated racks. The 40 mile range is enough to ride the causeway, catch the ferry and tour South Hero before heading home. They run 7 to 8 daily until overnight freezes arrive in November, and expansion spots are already under consideration.

[Sweetwaters Makes a Church Street Comeback in Burlington](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/firstbite/sweetwaters-makes-a-church-street-comeback-in-burlington/)

❝

*"There's nostalgia. There's history. People remember where they sat and what they ate," per Seven Days.*

Four years after closing to make way for Pascolo, David Melincoff reopened Sweetwaters in its old bank building at 120 Church Street, chicken fingers and Shirt Sleeve wrap intact alongside new dishes like a Queen City rice bowl that impressed the reviewer most. The 178 seat room hit a record 670 customers on a mid July Saturday, still shy of the original's thousand customer peaks. The 1981 mural of Bernie Sanders and Big Joe Burrell stays tiled over for now, though Melincoff says he would take a chisel to the tile himself if he could.

[In Chittenden Southeast Senate primary, incumbents and challengers alike pivot platforms to affordability](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/06/in-chittenden-southeast-senate-primary-incumbents-and-challengers-alike-pivot-platforms-to-affordability/)

❝

*"medium and large businesses are good for Vermont too," per VTDigger.*

Five Democrats are chasing three seats in Tuesday's primary for the district covering South Burlington, Shelburne, Williston and points southeast, with cross endorsed incumbents Thomas Chittenden, Ginny Lyons and Kesha Ram Hinsdale facing first time candidates Joanna Grossman and pediatrician Elizabeth Hunt. Healthcare affordability has displaced guns and climate as the issue voters raise at the door, with Lyons pointing to her work expanding primary care access and Hunt calling for an audit of the Green Mountain Care Board. Chittenden is running openly pro growth while Grossman stakes out small business ground, and the vote lands Tuesday, August 11.

[Burlington Dems Push Through Public Safety Resolution Despite Criticism](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-dems-push-through-public-safety-resolution-despite-criticism/)

❝

*"This resolution was developed to restore a simple expectation that everyone in Burlington feel safe," per Seven Days.*

The council's Democrats passed their Burlington Forward resolution 7 to 5 on party lines late Monday after more than 100 people spoke across three hours, 60 of them against. The measure targets unpermitted services like Food Not Cops' daily meal share in City Hall Park and calls for zoning that would keep needle exchanges and the planned overdose prevention center out of downtown. Mayor Mulvaney-Stanak had asked the Dems to table it and criticized them for skipping her office on the way in. It now heads to four committees, with reports due back September 14.

[Vermont student becomes first to attend national STEM competition](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/05/vermont-student-becomes-first-attend-national-stem-competition/)

❝

*"This showed me and hopefully can show the students that we're just as competitive as other states and that we can, you know, do projects at the same caliber," per WCAX.*

South Burlington rising senior Momin Waqar became Vermont's first ever representative at the national ACT-SO competition in Chicago, bringing a yearlong project on how an anti inflammatory peptide affects central nervous system cells, work that could sharpen understanding of diseases like ALS. He did part of the research in a UVM lab after winning the Vermont STEM fair in March, and the Rutland NAACP nominated him for the national stage. He starts senior year this fall hoping to keep the neuroscience going.

[BETA and EXIM Bank announce intent to expand financing for up to $1 billion](https://vermontbiz.com/news/2026/august/05/beta-and-exim-bank-announce-intent-expand-financing-1-billion)

❝

*"Through our 2025 IPO, BETA raised approximately $1.2 billion, and our relationship with EXIM Bank aims to provide an additional $1 billion of non-dilutive growth capital," per Vermont Business Magazine.*

The South Burlington electric aircraft maker and the Export-Import Bank announced intent to expand their financing relationship by up to $1 billion, including roughly $830 million in new capital on top of the $170 million that helped build the local production facility. BETA's commercial backlog has grown from about $1 billion to $3.9 billion since that first deal, and the money would go toward roughly doubling manufacturing throughput. Nothing is binding yet, and Q2 results land August 12.

[Burlington celebrates foolishness and amazing feats for 18th year](https://www.charlottenewsvt.org/2026/08/06/burlington-celebrates-foolishness-and-amazingfeats-for-18th-year/)

One couple drove up from Delaware for last weekend's Festival of Fools, which founder Woody Keppel dreamed up decades ago while passing through Burlington with a troupe of street performers. Street dancer Snap Boogie closed a Pine Street warmup show with at least seven one handed flips.

[Men's Soccer Ranked No. 13 in United Soccer Coaches Preseason Poll](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/8/4/mens-soccer-mens-soccer-ranked-no-13-in-united-soccer-coaches-preseason-poll.aspx)

UVM men's soccer opens the season ranked 13th nationally after last year's 29 match unbeaten run, the longest in the country. Niklas Herceg turned down FC Dallas to come back for another year, and the opener is Thursday, August 20 at Virtue Field against George Mason.

[Burlington mayor defends public safety policies](https://www.wamc.org/news/2026-08-05/burlington-mayor-defends-public-safety-policies)

Two days after the council advanced the Dems' safety plan, Mayor Mulvaney-Stanak used her monthly press conference to defend her administration's own approach, flanked by city staff and advocates. She plans to meet with Gov. Scott about state resources for homelessness and substance use.

[Sailboat catches fire in Malletts Bay after possible lightning strike](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/sailboat-catches-fire-in-malletts-bay-after-possible-lightning-strike/73372098)

Two people escaped unhurt after their sailboat caught fire on Malletts Bay Thursday, likely from a lightning strike to the mast. A nearby boater had it out with a fire extinguisher before Colchester crews even arrived.

[Man Admits to Paying Migrant Justice Leader in Border Smuggling Case](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/man-admits-to-paying-migrant-justice-leader-in-border-smuggling-case/)

Armando Paulino Estudillo pleaded guilty in federal court in Burlington to paying prominent immigrant rights organizer Nacho De La Cruz $2,000 to smuggle his girlfriend across the Canadian border last year. De La Cruz has pleaded not guilty to his own nine count indictment, and Paulino Estudillo faces sentencing in October.
