## Weather & Monday Update:

Grab the umbrella on your way out this morning, because Monday stays gray with on and off showers, mostly in the first half of the day, and a high right around 50. Tuesday starts dry before another round of showers rolls in for the afternoon and evening, with highs again near 50, and the two days only add up to a tenth or two of an inch. Veterans Day looks like the driest day of the week, mostly cloudy with maybe a peek of afternoon sun and a high in the upper 40s, and Wednesday night drops to about 31, so Thursday starts near freezing. Thursday stays cloudy and chilly in the mid 40s, and a storm could bring rain and some wet snow in the hills Friday into Saturday, though its track is still up in the air. Check in on [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html) any day this week for patio, swim, and sunset scores.

[Beta announces its new training center even as South Burlington ponders plan](https://www.vtcng.com/otherpapersbvt/news/business_news/beta-announces-its-new-training-center-even-as-south-burlington-ponders-plan/article_35937dd2-0020-4747-9dd7-4d88af58d99a.html)

❝

*"It’s really how Beta is going to be leaning into training pilots, training aircraft mechanics and really growing that programming in a meaningful way," Gagnon said, per The Other Paper.*

Beta Technologies has filed its first building project under its proposed master plan, a 61,000 square foot addition at 430 Davinci Drive that would create a four story training center and an adjoining aircraft hangar. The plans include six of Beta's full scale "ThunderDome" flight simulators, and Beta's Alex Gagnon says all the pilots for the company's electric fleet will train there. The project depends on the master plan, which South Burlington is still weighing with a decision due by Nov. 20, and Beta returns to the Development Review Board with revisions Nov. 17. If it's approved, Beta plans to finish in 2028, after a traffic light goes in at Williston Road and Davinci Drive.

[New Burlington police chief will face tall order](https://vtdigger.org/2026/11/01/new-burlington-police-chief-will-face-tall-order)

❝

*"We have half a police department right now," Bouchett said, per VTDigger.*

The City Council is expected to vote Monday, Nov. 9, on Mayor Emma Mulvaney-Stanak's pick for chief, Anthony Geraci, a 25 year law enforcement veteran who has served as police chief in Watervliet and Newburgh, New York. If the council approves him, [he starts Tuesday](https://www.wcax.com/2026/11/05/burlington-could-have-new-police-chief-soon-tuesday), with interim chief Shawn Burke staying until March to help with the transition. He would take over a department down to 54 officers with 19 vacancies. Hiring committee member Mark Bouchett of the Church Street Marketplace commission and Lake Champlain Chamber CEO Catherine Davis want recruiting to be a priority, while residents including former police commissioner Stephanie Seguino want a chief open to civilian oversight.

[City of Burlington invites public input on future development](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/city-of-burlington-vt-invites-public-input-on-future-development/74352078)

❝

*"I would love to see a second floor built on the Hannaford's Plaza or a third floor even built there," Eley said, per NBC5.*

Residents came to Burlington High School last Thursday to weigh in on PlanBTV2050, the city's plan for new recreation, safer infrastructure for cyclists and a 7,000 unit housing goal, including where those units should go. Lifelong resident Emile Eley doesn't want development on public lands, and Joel Hakken said the 100 unit homeless shelter should be the immediate priority. The next public meeting on the plan won't happen until the new year.

City Council President Ben Traverse, a Democrat who first won the South End's Ward 5 seat in 2022, launched his campaign for mayor Friday at the Battery Park pavilion, setting up a possible race against Progressive Mayor Emma Mulvaney-Stanak, who says she'll announce her reelection plans in the next few days. [Despite $928 Million Spent, Lake Champlain Cleanup Is Falling Behind](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/investigation/lake-champlain-cleanup-falling-behind)

❝

*"Halfway through the two-decade timetable, Vermont has achieved only 35 percent of the goal, according to the state’s most recent estimates," per Seven Days.*

A Seven Days and ProPublica investigation finds Vermont's Lake Champlain cleanup faltering more than a decade after the 2015 Clean Water Act was signed on the Burlington waterfront, and the EPA warned last year the state would need to more than double its pace to hit the 2036 target. Seven of the lake's 15 sampling stations have topped EPA phosphorus limits every year since 2015, with agriculture the largest polluting sector, as state agencies have repeatedly delayed tougher action on farms, businesses and housing developments. Agency of Natural Resources Secretary Julie Moore says the projects are on pace even though the lake data is slower to show it. A [companion story](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/investigation/mandated-audit-lake-cleanup-efforts-never-done) found the outside audit lawmakers mandated for the $928 million spent since 2016 never happened because no firms bid, and Sen. ❝
