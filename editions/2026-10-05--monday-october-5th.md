## Weather & Monday Update:

If you still have tomatoes or potted herbs out on the porch, this is the week to bring them inside. Monday turns breezy and cooler behind this morning's cold front, with a high around 58, gusts near 30, and a chance of a passing shower this afternoon. Tuesday is sunny but chilly, topping out around 55, and both Monday and Tuesday nights dip to around 39 in town, with Wednesday morning the best shot at frost. Wednesday bounces back to around 61 with plenty of sun and a gusty south wind, and Thursday is the warmest day of the stretch, with a high near 64 and a chance of showers later in the day. [Vermont Historical Society acquires Big Heavy World’s music collection](https://vermontbiz.com/news/2026/october/03/vermont-historical-society-acquires-big-heavy-worlds-music-collection)

❝

*"The collection also contains physical items related to Vermont’s music scene, such as the ladle from Nectar’s and the door from 242 Main," per Vermont Business Magazine.*

James Lockridge founded Big Heavy World in 1996, and for nearly two decades it saved interviews, radio shows, recordings, performances and posters from the state's music scene, including performance recordings of Phish, Anaïs Mitchell, Grace Potter and the Nocturnals, Big Joe Burrell and the Vermont Symphony Orchestra at the Flynn. Big Heavy World closed its space in 2025, and Lockridge offered the collection to the Vermont Historical Society, which will fold it into its existing holdings and make it accessible to scholars, musicians and listeners. VHS Director of Collections and Access Amanda Kay Gustin says the collection will have a home with the society for the benefit of future generations.

[Charlie’s Boathouse Property to Be Conserved, Turned Into Park](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/charlies-boathouse-property-to-be-conserved-turned-into-park)

❝

*"It’s a real high priority for us to get people on there as quickly as possible," per Seven Days.*

The Winooski Valley Park District bought the 2.7 acre parcel at the very end of North Avenue for $960,000, along with the whole fleet of kayaks and historic photos, and the Lake Champlain Land Trust holds the conservation easement. Charlie Auer Jr. and his sister, Christine Auer Hebert, ran a bait shop, snack bar and boat rental there for decades until it closed when Charlie died in 2021, and the families listed the property last fall for $2.5 million. It stays closed to the public for now, with an opening probably sometime in the spring.

[Anti-ICE activists plan week of community engagement, learning and protest](https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/02/anti-ice-activists-plan-week-of-community-engagement-learning-and-protest)

❝

*"It’s not typically known that, you know, two-thirds of the surveillance infrastructure for ICE nationally is just in this one town of 10,000 people," per VTDigger.*

Shut Down ICE VT's week of action, Oct. 4 to 9, aims to halt operations at Williston's two ICE facilities, the Law Enforcement Support Center, home to the agency's national tip line, and the National Criminal Analysis and Targeting Center in White Cap Business Park. The week mixes civil disobedience with skills trainings, art activities and community meals, and the biggest public event is a rally at the Law Enforcement Support Center on Thursday, Oct. 8, from 10 to 11:30 a.m. Attorney General Charity Clark recently charged 13 protesters arrested at the White Cap facility in July, a move Gov. Phil Scott praised, after Chittenden County State's Attorney Sarah George repeatedly declined to press charges against ICE protesters.

[Guard commander says Vermont F-35 pilots faced hostile fire over Iran](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/02/guard-commander-says-vermont-f-35-pilots-faced-hostile-fire-over-iran)

❝

*"It was an impactful, stressful, difficult deployment," per WCAX.*

Vermont National Guard Commander Maj. Gen. Hank Harder told WCAX that the Green Mountain Boys were tasked with taking out Iranian air defenses during their recent Middle East deployment and came under attack, though the Guard took no casualties. A dozen F-35s deployed and most are back, with a handful left behind with the unit that replaced them, and Harder says none were completely lost but wouldn't say whether any were damaged, citing operational security. The Guard is now offering returning airmen and women mental health resources and other help transitioning back to civilian life.

[Medicaid Changes Are Set to Kick In for Vermonters](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/healthcare/medicaid-changes-are-set-to-kick-in-for-vermonters)

❝

*"In Vermont, we have been focused on affordability, and these policy changes move us in the opposite direction," per Seven Days.*

An estimated 235 Vermonters were expected to lose Medicaid on October 1 over restrictions tied to immigration status, the first wave of changes from the federal budget law President Donald Trump signed last year, and starting January 1, work requirements and twice yearly reviews phase in for an estimated 49,000 low income adults as they renew. Most can qualify by showing at least $580 a month in household income, 80 hours a month of work, education or community service, or half time enrollment as a student, with exceptions for pregnant people, some parents and caregivers, and the medically frail. Health care advocate Mike Fisher expects most people to meet the rules and worries more about eligible Vermonters getting dropped because they can't keep up with the extra paperwork and deadlines. At the state's Ferrisburgh town hall on the changes, not a single Medicaid enrollee showed up.

[Federal court says U.S. agents can search phones at Vermont’s border with Canada without suspicion](https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/01/federal-court-says-us-border-agents-can-search-phones-at-vermonts-border-with-canada-without-suspicion)

❝

*"Because a cellphone is property, our precedents establish that the government does not need to have reasonable suspicion before searching a traveler’s cellphone at the border," per VTDigger.*

The 2nd Circuit ruling, written by Judge Steven J. Menashi, applies at every port of entry in the court's jurisdiction, including Vermont's border with Canada, and covers manual searches, which generally means an agent scrolling through your phone while you're present. If you won't hand it over unlocked, officials could confiscate it and extract data with digital tools, says Scott Wilkens of the Knight First Amendment Institute, and the judges didn't rule on whether that deeper search needs any suspicion. Wilkens worries the ruling could embolden more searches in Vermont, and he says the Supreme Court will likely take up the issue in some capacity in the coming years.

[Record diesel prices strain Vermont school district budgets](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/02/record-diesel-prices-strain-vermont-school-district-budgets)

❝

*"We didn’t budget another 80-plus-thousand dollars, you know, in fuel," per WCAX.*

That's Jon Mabee, transportation director for South Burlington Public Schools, and with AAA putting Vermont diesel at $6.40 a gallon, the Champlain Valley School District is on track to spend $200,000 more than budgeted, about $16,000 to $20,000 extra a month. South Burlington's buses travel a combined 300,000 miles a year at 5 to 7 miles to the gallon, and its 6 all electric buses are limited by range. If prices keep rising, the district may cancel trips and ask parents to drive students to athletic events.

[1 in 5 Vermonters say they have struggled to keep up with rent and mortgage payments](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-01/vermonters-struggled-rent-mortgage-payments)

❝

*"Sometimes it’s choosing between, you know, medical necessities for my kids, or gas in the car, or food," per Vermont Public.*

In the Vermont Public 2026 Poll, 20% of people surveyed in September said they had been late or come up short on rent or mortgage payments in the past year, and another 12% almost fell behind. Younger Vermonters are hit hardest, with 27% of those 18 to 44 struggling compared with 9% of people over 65. The median primary home sold for $379,000 last year, up more than 91% from a decade earlier, and the share of Vermont renters who could afford a median priced home fell from nearly a third in 2021 to 6% two years later.

[Burlington YMCA property redevelopment moves one step closer to new housing](https://www.wcax.com/2026/09/30/burlington-ymca-property-redevelopment-moves-one-step-closer-new-housing)

❝

*"This floor is just something we had to preserve, it’s from Shelburne and Redstone quarries. Local stonemasons in 1925 put this together," per WCAX.*

Green Castle Group's Bruce Baker is turning the old downtown Y, vacant from 2019 until construction began in 2025, into 79 apartments from studios to four bedrooms, each with its own heating and cooling, plus a 24 hour gym, balconies and community spaces. Crews are wrapping up exterior finishes and working on interiors from the top floor down, aiming to get the last utility connections in before winter. Construction should finish next spring, 20% of the units are already leased, and residents are on track to move in June 1, 2027.

[Vermont’s Housing Market Has Tilted Back Toward Buyers, But Only a Certain Kind](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/home-design/realestate/vermonts-housing-market-has-tilted-back-toward-buyers-but-only-a-certain-kind)

❝

*"It doesn’t seem like that Easter-egg mentality we saw during peak COVID, where it felt like whoever found it first was going to get it," per Seven Days.*

The Vermont Association of Realtors counted nearly 3,000 active listings statewide last month, the most since 2020, and the median home now spends 17 days on the market, up from a low of 6 in 2022, so even in Chittenden County homes with more than a few offers are the exception. Buyers are back to demanding inspections and appraisals, and realtors are advising sellers to cut asking prices if offers don't come within the first couple of weeks. Agents don't expect prices to fall, with Vermont's median at $450,000 as of August, which means covering a $2,800 monthly mortgage and coming up with a $90,000 down payment at 20%.

[Campaign Countdown: Where do Scott and Janoo stand on 2nd home tax?](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/01/campaign-countdown-where-do-scott-janoo-stand-2nd-home-tax)

❝

*"I don’t know how many more rich people we have to lose in Vermont, but we’ve lost quite a few," per WCAX.*

A Vermont Public poll found 57% approve of taxing second homes and 28% oppose, and Democrat Amanda Janoo says the tax could free up housing for Vermonters, with the highest rates aimed at private equity and large corporate owners. Gov. Phil Scott signed off on a new second home tax classification as part of a legislative compromise but calls the push a knee jerk reaction by the Legislature, and both candidates agree any new tax must exclude deer camps or camps owned by working Vermonters. About 14% of the state's housing stock, or 50,000 homes, counts as seasonally vacant, and the Legislature has not yet set a rate.

## UVM Athletics: Women's Hockey Shuts Out No. 7 Duluth as Swimming Makes History

Women's hockey opened its season by [shutting out No. 7 Minnesota Duluth 3-0](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/womens-ice-hockey-womens-hockey-opens-regular-season-play-with-3-0-shutout-victory-over-7-minnesota-duluth.aspx) on Friday morning at Gutterson. Morgann Skoda, Stella Retrum and Kaylee Lewis scored, and Tara Bach made 36 saves in her Catamount debut for her first shutout in green and gold. It was only the second season opening win over a ranked opponent in program history, the other a 3-1 win over No. 7 North Dakota in 2014. Saturday's rematch [went to overtime](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/3/womens-ice-hockey-womens-hockey-and-7-minnesota-duluth-concludes-opening-weekend-series-in-overtime.aspx), where Josie St. Martin scored exactly one minute in to give the Bulldogs a 3-2 win. Natalie Zarcone scored and Hilary Wilkin got her first career goal in the loss. The Cats are 1-1 and play at Franklin Pierce on Friday at 7 and Saturday at 4, streamed on FloCollege.

The men [came back to beat Brock 4-2](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/4/mens-ice-hockey-mens-hockey-tops-brock-in-exhibition-opener-on-uvm-weekend.aspx) from 2-0 down in Saturday night's exhibition at Gutterson. Logan Renkowski and Ethan Burroughs tied it in the second, Andoni Fimis put Vermont ahead with a wrist shot 5:23 into the third, and Michael Hagens hit the empty net with 21 seconds left. The regular season starts Friday at 7 at Bentley on FloHockey.TV and WVMT, and [Boston University visits the Gut](https://uvmathletics.com/sports/mens-ice-hockey/schedule) on October 16 and 17 at 7 on ESPN+.

No. 12 men's soccer [drew 1-1 with NJIT](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/3/mens-soccer-no-12-mens-soccer-settles-for-1-1-draw-against-njit.aspx) on Saturday at Virtue Field and stayed unbeaten at home at 5-0-2. Diogo Rebelo put the Highlanders ahead in the fifth minute, and Philipp Schmidtke answered in the 60th off a David Ismail through ball for his fourth goal of the season. The women [beat Maine 2-1](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/4/womens-soccer-defeats-maine-2-1-at-home-sunday-afternoon.aspx) on Sunday, and the Black Bears were [picked first in the America East preseason poll](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/womens-soccer-hosts-maine-for-eastrise-jersey-giveaway-on-sunday.aspx). Madeline Gardner scored in the 27th minute off a Sophia Kling goal kick, Kling's first collegiate assist, and then set up Eleni Stowell's fifth goal of the year in the 63rd to move the Cats to 2-0-1 in conference play. Both teams travel this weekend, the men to UMBC on Saturday at 7 and the women to Bryant on Sunday at 1, both on ESPN+.

Field hockey [lost its America East opener 1-0 in overtime](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/field-hockey-drops-america-east-opener-to-new-hampshire-in-overtime.aspx) at New Hampshire on Friday. Vermont answered by [routing Queens 8-0](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/4/field-hockey-cruises-past-queens-8-0-on-sunday-afternoon.aspx) at home on Sunday. Alicia Battistelli scored a hat trick, her second of the season, and Veerle Nijhuis and Fiona McMahon scored twice each. Queens never got a shot off, Vermont took 38, and its 18 penalty corners set a program record. The Cats host UMass Lowell on Friday at 3 in the America East Spread Respect game on AmericaEast.TV, then Appalachian State on Sunday at 11, both during [alumni weekend](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/3/field-hockey-hosts-queens-sunday-for-second-all-time-meeting.aspx).

Swimming and diving [beat Northeastern for the first time in program history](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/3/womens-swimming-and-diving-catamounts-earn-first-ever-win-over-northeastern-as-they-celebrate-50th-anniversary.aspx), 158-123, on Saturday at Forbush Natatorium, after starting [0-15 all time against the Huskies](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/womens-swimming-and-diving-catamounts-host-northeastern-in-50th-anniversary-meet-saturday-of-uvm-weekend.aspx). Vermont won 10 events, two each from Ariel Indrisano in the breaststroke and Robyn Tonomura-MacDonald, who swept the diving for the first time in her career. The program's [50th anniversary team store](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/womens-swimming-and-diving-catamounts-host-northeastern-in-50th-anniversary-meet-saturday-of-uvm-weekend.aspx) is open through October 12. Cross country runs the [New England Championships](https://uvmathletics.com/sports/mens-cross-country/schedule) in Hopkinton, N.H., on Friday and hosts the Fall Foliage Invite in St. Albans on October 18, and men's basketball plays a [home exhibition against Holy Cross](https://uvmathletics.com/sports/mens-basketball/schedule) on October 17 at 4. And on Friday night the [UVM Athletic Hall of Fame](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/10/2/general-uvm-athletics-enshrines-hall-of-fame-class-of-2026-friday-night.aspx) inducted seven honorees at the Davis Center.
