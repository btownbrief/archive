## Weather & Monday Update:

Get your outdoor errands done early today. Monday starts comfortable and partly sunny with a high near 83, then scattered showers may roll in through the late afternoon and turn likely in the evening. Tuesday is the reward, mostly sunny and drier with a high near 82. Wednesday keeps the good thing going with sunshine, lower humidity and a high near 83 before a stray evening storm chance creeps back. Thursday is the washout of the week, showers and storms most of the day with a high near 80, and behind it comes a cooler, sunnier stretch into the weekend. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

Monday itself has some quiet gems. Partizanfilm on College Street runs a one night only screening of [WTO/99](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/509411) at 6:20, an archival documentary on the 40,000 strong 1999 Seattle protests against the World Trade Organization. Language lovers can drop into [Spanish Chat Mondays](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/507129) at Three Needs from 7 to 8, all levels welcome. If you'd rather move than talk, [pickup dodgeball](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/498094) runs 7 to 8:15 at the Miller Center for $5 a game, no team needed. Readers can join the [Book Club For Curious Women](https://www.meetup.com/vtladiessocialgroup/events/315459812/) [MEETUP] in Essex Junction at 6:30, where the whole month's homework is two chapters. And the [Collage Collective](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/collage-collective/4207/) gathers at Expressive Arts on Flynn Ave from 6:30 to 9, by donation, beginners welcome.

Tuesday is primary day in Vermont, so vote before you play. Polls run all day, and if an absentee ballot is still sitting on your counter the advice at this point is to hand it to your town clerk in person rather than mail it, more on that in the news below. The big musical event of the week also starts Tuesday, with [Lake Street Dive](https://highergroundmusic.com/events/lake-street-dive/) opening a two night stand on the Shelburne Museum green at 7. Both nights are sold out, so this one is for ticket holders and wait list optimists, and kids 12 and under get in free without a ticket. Otherwise Tuesday leans social. Runners can lace up for the [5K at Queen City Brewery](https://www.meetup.com/getting-active-in-burlington/events/316003364/) [MEETUP] at 5:30 with a beverage waiting at the finish, early birds can try the free [morning networking hour](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/morning-networking-45295444) at Hula at 8:30 with coffee on the house, and beginners with a sewing machine gathering dust can bring it to the free [Sandbox sewing class](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/sandbox-free-sewing-class-for-beginners/5596/) on Pine Street from 4 to 5:30. [Traditional Tuesday](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/traditional-tuesday/6325/) at Mesmer Kava pours all-you-like kava shells for a $20 flat fee from 4:30 to 8, [Super Smash Tuesdays](https://calendar.time.ly/vfaca7kw/event/78077209/20260811170000) takes over RíRá from 5 with free play and a $10 smash burger, and French speakers get [Pause-Café Mardi](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/pause-cafe-mardi-44550535) at Burlington Bay from 5 to 7.

Wednesday stacks up nicely. [Lake Street Dive](https://highergroundmusic.com/events/lake-street-dive-2/) plays night two at Shelburne Museum, and downtown answers with [Art Amplified](https://www.facebook.com/events/852942457240479/), Burlington City Arts' free evening of live music with Troy Millette and the Fire Below and Collin Hagood at the BCA Center from 5. Comedy people have a full evening at Vermont Comedy Club, with the [$5 Improv Night](https://www-vermontcomedyclub-com.seatengine.com/shows/380765) at 7 featuring Big Green Couch and an open jam after the show. Also on Wednesday, Local Motion is hosting the [Ride for Shiloh at 5:30](https://www.localmotion.org/ride_for_shiloh). The community Critical Mass ride will meet at the memorial on the corner of Pine and Locust Streets to honor the 16-year-old BHS student whose tragic passing we’ve already covered. It’s a chance to support her family, remember her legacy, and stand together in support of safer streets for everyone. The Media Factory runs [Audio Recording 101](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/audio-recording-101-45372023) from 6 to 8, a free hands-on tour of microphones and recording basics. [Trivia](https://www.facebook.com/events/2911940769198427/) lands at the Venetian Soda Lounge on Pine Street at 7, and artists can bring a sketchbook to Daydream Art Supply's [Summer Sketchbook Club](https://www.facebook.com/events/1671594150773089/) on Church Street from 5. Our own [Pick-up Basketball](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/316010961/) [MEETUP] runs at Pomeroy Park from 5:30, free and outdoors, come get a run in.

Thursday is the fullest day on the calendar, so keep half an eye on the sky with storms in the forecast. [Party On The Bricks](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/party-on-the-bricks/7524/) throws its season finale on the Leahy Block from 5:30 to 8 with Ali T playing outside Ken's Pizza and Pub, free and open to all. South Burlington's [SB Nite Out](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/504478) fills Veterans Memorial Park with food trucks and live music from 5:30 to 8, Williston gets a free [John Fogerty tribute](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/the-maple-tree-place-concert-on-the-green-presenting-the-john-fogerty-tribute-project/7779/) on the Maple Tree Place green at 6, and cyclists can head to the [Bivo Summer Bash](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/508500) at Cochran's in Richmond from 5 to 8:30, with group rides followed by live music, sourdough pizza and Frost beers. Film lovers can celebrate Alfred Hitchcock's 127th birthday with [Notorious](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/notorious/7785/) at the VTIFF Screening Room at 7, and theater people get opening night of [Falsettos ($14-35)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/falsettos-45337843) at the Black Box Theater at 7, running through August 23. [Music bingo](https://www.facebook.com/events/4517729075142611/) plays at Sparky's in Essex Junction at 6:30, the Odd Fellows hall on North Ave hosts [figure drawing with the Emanata Art Collective](https://www.facebook.com/events/990415543765906/) at 7, and night owls can two-step into the [Thursday Night Social Club ($10, 21+)](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/thursday-night-social-club-andrea-maddox-%26-the-hey-yalls/7768/) at Radio Bean, a 10:30 show from Americana six piece Andrea Maddox & The Hey Y'alls. We'll also be at [Trivia at Four Quarters](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/316025610/) [MEETUP] in Winooski at 6, come early and help us hold a table.

## Local News (All Links Clickable)

[Data: Only 3% of Vermont registered voters have cast their ballots ahead of the primary election](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/07/data-only-3-of-vermont-registered-voters-have-cast-their-ballots-ahead-of-the-primary-election/)

❝

*"August is a time of, you know, summer vacation and family trips and relatives from out of town, and it's a hard time for people to tune in," per VTDigger.*

Tomorrow is primary day, and as of late last week only 3 percent of registered Vermont voters had returned a ballot. Secretary of State Sarah Copeland Hanzas is publishing early turnout numbers for the first time as a nudge, with Shelburne among the few towns beating the average. The practical part for Monday readers is that it is now too late to trust the mail, so hand your absentee ballot to your town clerk or just vote in person Tuesday.

[Mirabelles Bakery to Take Over Both Kitchen Table Locations](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/foodnews/mirabelles-bakery-to-take-over-both-kitchen-table-locations/)

❝

*"We would not be able to do this without her skill set and talents," per Seven Days.*

Both Kitchen Table spots closed earlier this month, and Mirabelles is taking over the pair, the restaurant in Richmond and the market on Williston Road in South Burlington, with reopenings targeted by the end of September. The Richmond spot will likely become 'Belles on the River, serving casual dinners Wednesday through Sunday plus weekend brunch with the popovers along for the ride. One thing worth watching is that Beta Technologies bought the building housing the current Mirabelles bakery, so that location's long-term future is open.

[South End residents suggest ways to make biking safer, following teen death in e-bike crash](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/south-end-residents-biking-safer-teen-death-crash/73360891)

❝

*"Everyone has a legal right to ride their bikes on these streets, and motorists need to be aware of that and respect that," per NBC5.*

Local Motion held its first walk-bike forum steps from the Pine Street memorial for 16 year old Shiloh Skalka, who died in July after being struck while riding her e-bike. Neighbors marked dangerous intersections on city maps and pitched fixes like new crosswalks, with Councilor Becca Brown McKnight calling the moment a wake-up call and saying the city is not doing enough. The driver has been cited and is expected in court in late August, and the Department of Public Works says the input feeds directly into long-range street planning.

[Athletes at the Vermont Ninja Warrior Training Center Aim for the Big Time](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/arts-culture/sports/athletes-at-the-vermont-ninja-warrior-training-center-aim-for-the-big-time/)

❝

*"People underestimate our athletes a lot of the time," per Seven Days.*

The Vermont Ninja Warrior Training Center moved into its own 7,200 square foot gym in Essex this spring, and three of its own are on the current season of American Ninja Warrior, including comanager Amir Malik of Essex Junction, who made the National Finals. About 300 students train there weekly, with kids starting as young as 4 and a 44 athlete competitive team that has traveled as far as Barbados for championships. The sport joins the Olympics in 2028, so the pipeline running through Corporate Drive suddenly has a very big ceiling.

[Ray's Seafood Market and Restaurant closing after 74 years](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/08/rays-seafood-market-restaurant-closing-after-74-years/)

❝

*"Used to come here and bring my parents here years ago, they enjoyed it... So we'll miss it because it's really good," per WCAX.*

Ray's Seafood in Essex Junction announced this weekend it will close after 74 years, with the last day of service set for September 12. The family business started as a wholesale seafood provider 74 years ago and gave no reason for the decision. There is a month left to get a final basket of fried clams, and if you have a gift receipt the ask is to use it before the doors close.

[One month in, Champlain Parkway draws both praise and complaints](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/04/one-month-champlain-parkway-draws-both-praise-complaints/)

❝

*"It's so much easier to just get from here right onto the highway. So, yeah, it's been fantastic for us," per WCAX.*

Five weeks in, drivers love how fast the Parkway gets them to the highway, and Pine Street is paying for it with new backups. The top complaint is the missing left turn arrow where the project swapped four-way stops for signals at King and Maple, with waits of up to three light cycles at peak times. DPW is collecting signal data and owes the City Council findings and recommendations within six months.

[Chefs and servers on the Champlain Valley Dinner Train keep service on track](https://vtcommunitynews.org/2026/08/04/chefs-and-servers-on-the-champlain-valley-dinner-train-keep-service-on-track/)

❝

*"This is a full restaurant kitchen where it's done just like it's done in the restaurant. So that sets us apart from a lot of different dinner trains," per Community News Service.*

Community News Service rode along in the kitchen car of the Champlain Valley Dinner Train, where four cooks plated nearly 170 three course dinners on a single evening run. The train now rolls all the way to Middlebury on a three hour round trip from Burlington, with Rutland's Southside Steakhouse catering as it has since 2018 and servers logging up to 25,000 steps a shift. The season runs through October if you want the moving-dining-room experience yourself.

[Queen City Public Safety Debate Shifts to Needle Exchanges](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/topic/burlington/queen-city-public-safety-debate-shifts-to-needle-exchanges/)

❝

*"Burlington's got issues that we need to work on, but it definitely feels like people are looking for a scapegoat," per Seven Days.*

The downtown safety fight has narrowed onto harm reduction, after the council's Burlington Forward resolution passed 7 to 5 with zoning language aimed at keeping syringe programs and the planned overdose prevention center out of downtown. The flashpoint is Vermont CARES' expanded Bank Street exchange, which opened July 1 with state backing after the Howard Center's Clarke Street program closed under a neighborhood lawsuit. Business owners and developers are lining up behind the Democrats while Progressives and the pastor who rents to Vermont CARES argue the exchanges are being scapegoated for a drug trade that predates them. Council committees take the measure up next, with a full council look in September, so this one is far from settled.

[A chastened UVM Health embarks on its first budget season after leadership reset](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-08-05/uvm-health-first-budget-season-after-leadership-reset)

❝

*"What I hope you see in this budget is not business as usual, not more of the same," per Vermont Public.*

UVM Health is back before the Green Mountain Care Board with a message of contrition, pointing to a new CEO, executive cuts, paused bonuses, a 12 percent price reduction in its BlueCross contract and dozens of layoffs. The system still projects a $14.7 million loss next year, with the Burlington flagship down $43 million, and its proposed expense growth remains more than triple what regulators suggested. The board's overseer wants another $50 million cut, and budget orders are due by September 15.

[Burlington mid-year housing market review](https://www.caledonianrecord.com/news/state/burlington-mid-year-housing-market-review/article_08b96097-e583-58bd-ac73-075a122ae096.html)

❝

*"Seven months into 2026, the housing market is showing signs of unwinding from the pandemic-era frenzy that drained supply, pushed prices to record highs, and brought activity to a standstill," per the Caledonian Record.*

The mid-year Redfin numbers show the Burlington market finally exhaling, with the median sale price down 4.2 percent from last year while the national figure rose. Active listings are up 19.4 percent, homes now sit a median 48 days before selling, and the share going above asking has slipped. Nobody would call $466,000 affordable, but after years of frenzy, more choices and longer decision windows are a real shift for buyers.

[Majestic Chef Micah Tavelli to Leave Burlington for a Big-City Gig](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/majestic-chef-micah-tavelli-to-leave-burlington-for-a-big-city-gig/)

❝

*"I don't think I'd be the man or the chef I am today if not for living and learning here," per Seven Days.*

Micah Tavelli, the James Beard semifinalist behind the stoves at Majestic in the South End, is leaving to run the kitchen at a Danny Meyer group restaurant opening in Boston's Seaport. His last day is this Saturday, so the week ahead is your window for one more Tavelli-era dinner. Dan Schloemer, his former sous chef, takes over with the menu expected to hold steady through the fall.

[Healthy Living appeals labor board decision upholding employee union](https://www.vtcng.com/otherpapersbvt/news/business_news/healthy-living-appeals-labor-board-decision-upholding-employee-union/article_79085183-30e2-40fc-9a9f-3d02d0c34682.html)

❝

*"There's no evidence for any of this, and this is sort of the same tactic that we see a lot of the time when some entity doesn't like what's going on, and they accuse everybody of being an outside agitator," per The Other Paper.*

Healthy Living has filed a roughly 700 page appeal asking the national labor board to throw out its workers' 105 to 69 April union vote, alleging a coordinated infiltration campaign by organizers. Two of the named baristas were fired the day before a union press conference, which the company attributes to guest complaints and the workers attribute to organizing. Salting is legal and protected under labor board precedent, and a decision could take three months or more.

[Annual recovery summit aims to explain the unexplainable](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/06/annual-recovery-summit-aims-to-explain-the-unexplainable/)

❝

*"It feels like a family reunion," per VTDigger.*

The fifth annual Coming Together, There is Hope summit drew an estimated 2,000 people to the Champlain Valley Expo at the end of July, including the lieutenant governor and a federal judge speaking on addiction in her courtroom. Vermont carries some of the highest substance use rates in the country, and about 70 percent of people incarcerated here receive medication assisted treatment. The message from organizers, both in long-term recovery themselves, was that recovery is a community project rather than a solo one.
