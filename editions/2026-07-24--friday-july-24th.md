## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

*Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at*[Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html)*.*

If you have been waiting all month for a flawless summer weekend, this is the one. Friday is sunny with a high right around 84, low humidity, and a light breeze off the south, and the evening stays clear straight through sunset at 8:30 for anything you have planned outside. Saturday is the warm one, sunny and pushing 88 in the Champlain Valley while the air stays comfortable, with nights settling into the upper 50s and mid 60s. Sunday keeps the sunshine at around 86, with only the smallest chance a stray shower sneaks in late in the afternoon. Rain and storms hold off until Monday afternoon, so wring every drop out of this stretch.

Friday night has serious range. The big one is ["Weird Al" Yankovic ($69.50, kids 12 and under free)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/higher-ground-presents-weird-al-yankovic-45316802) on the Midway Lawn at the Champlain Valley Expo in Essex Junction at 7:30, with [Puddles Pity Party](https://www.facebook.com/events/862318116461962/) opening, an outdoor evening of accordion mischief and sad clown crooning that should be something to behold. Comedy fans staying downtown can catch [Jordan Jensen](https://www.facebook.com/events/784070187510418/) at the Flynn at 7:30, fresh off her Netflix special "Take Me With You". Music people have choices all over the map, with Seven Daysies winning cover band [Sticks and Stones](https://www.facebook.com/events/1441175171100900/) playing the Old Post from 7 to 10, songwriter [Jeffrey Foucault](https://www.facebook.com/events/1548752720292251/) doing an early all ages show in the Light Club Lamp Shop room at Radio Bean at 7, and the [Zach Nugent Duo](https://www.facebook.com/events/1405889134902876/) bringing free Grateful Dead energy to the Skinny Pancake patio at 8 for their Dancing in the Streets series. Theater folks should note [Steal Fire's "No Exit"](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/505483) at Off Center, a new multimedia staging of the Sartre classic at 7:30 tonight with a French existentialism talkback, plus 2 PM matinees Saturday and Sunday.

Earlier in the day Friday works too. The [South Burlington Farmers Market](https://www.facebook.com/events/1021963913505317/) runs its Friday evening window from 5 to 7 on Airport Drive, [Sisters of Anarchy](https://www.facebook.com/events/1019562877260405/) in Shelburne hosts Food Truck Friday from 5 to 8 with live music from Tom Bisson, Nameless Tacos, and farm grown ice cream for dessert, and downtown lunch breaks get free old time folk from [Dana and Susan Robinson](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/summer-concert-series-dana-and-susan-robinson-45149264) in City Hall Park at 12:30.

Saturday belongs to the Old North End. [The Ramble](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/the-ramble-45341388) takes over the neighborhood from 8 in the morning to 10 at night with over 50 DIY pop-ups, including homemade waffle gardens on Manhattan Drive, a build-your-own bouquet bar at Burly Blooms, and driveway chess with "janky bleachers" on Oak Street. Neighbors will be out all day for front-porch art galleries, sidewalk lemonade stands, street-wide yard sales, the 4:15 PM community bike ride, and the evening Ramble Round Up beer garden. So be sure to come out and enjoy Burlington’s Old North End. The [ONE World Market](https://www.facebook.com/events/1340677511602697/) folds right into it from 11 to 3 on Allen Street with dozens of local vendors. Downtown, the [BTV Market](https://www.facebook.com/events/1713981662929469/) sets up outside City Hall from 11 to 3, [DJ CRWD CNTL spins Splash Dance](https://www.facebook.com/events/1534057181690358/) by the City Hall Park fountain from 1 to 3, and the [Burlington Farmers Market](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/489381) does its 70 vendor thing on Pine Street from 9 to 2. Our own [BTown Coffee Club [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/315557313/) meets Saturday at 10 at Zero Gravity, big table on the right, newcomers always welcome, and then the crew heads to [Paddle at Oakledge Beach [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/315470651/) at 11 for a couple easygoing hours of kayaking and paddleboarding on the lake. Bound to be a great morning so come say hi.

Saturday afternoon and evening keep rolling. Skaters can escape the heat at the [Free Family Skate](https://www.facebook.com/events/1823469395028001/) at Cairns Arena from 1:30 to 3 with $5 rentals, the [Greek Mini-Festival](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/greek-mini-festival-45306891) brings souvlaki, spanakopita, and baklava to the Dormition Greek Orthodox Church lawn from 4 to 8, and the [free community Summer BBQ on North Ave](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/497899) serves up burgers, hot dogs, live music, and yard games from 4 to 7. Kids in the Hall legend Kevin McDonald does two different shows at Vermont Comedy Club, his comic rock opera ["Superstar" at 7 ($25)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/kevin-mcdonald-superstar-45337405) and the improvisers shoving him onstage for ["Forced to Improvise" at 8:30 ($15)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/kevin-mcdonald-is-being-forced-to-improvise-45337406). Punk fans get Sub Pop provocateurs [Downtown Boys at Foam Brewers](https://www.facebook.com/events/2508497122958203/) at 8:30, Colchester gets high energy covers from [Magnetic Horse at the Spanked Puppy](https://www.facebook.com/events/1020215057607908/) at 7, tabletop people can hit the [Board Game Brunch [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/friendlytabletopgamers/events/315556504/) at Old Town Cafe & Comics in Williston at 9, and anyone curious about basics can take the free 45 minute [community self defense class](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/community-self-defense-class-45281852) at Oom Yung Doe on North Ave at 6.

Sunday is a gem. Winooski crowns its [Best Pooch in the Noosk](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/best-pooch-in-the-noosk-45023083) at the [Winooski Farmers Market](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/winooski-farmers-market-45023077), both running 10 to 2 at Winooski Falls Way, and every dog in the Onion City is invited to compete for titles like Cutest. Our group's [Centennial Hills Hike and Winooski Circle [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/315226295/) meets at Schmanska Park at 11 for a loop through Centennial Woods followed by an optional beer at the Winooski Circle, dogs welcome, and with a crowd this size you will make a friend or five. Craft types can build pebble art under the pavilion at [Sam Mazza's in Colchester](https://www.facebook.com/events/4251776041711515/) from 10 to 12 with proceeds supporting Camp Ta-Kum-Ta, brunch people have the [Blueberry Jam Brunch](https://www.facebook.com/events/1176829594629135/) at the Swaying Daisies cafe at Isham Family Farm in Williston from 11 to 3, and the morning starts gently with the string ensemble [Skylark playing Sunday Classical](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/sunday-classical-skylark-45149269) in the BCA Center gallery on Church Street at 10. The Safety Team runs a 2.5 hour [Empowerment Self-Defense Workshop](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/505526) at the South Burlington Community Room at 10, taught by women for women with scholarships available. Later on, [Déjà Nous plays jazz and swing at Shelburne Vineyard](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/deja-nous-45310961) at 1 for free, [Womxn's pick-up soccer [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/womens-soccer-vermont/events/315787640/) kicks off at Starr Farm Park at 5, and the weekend closes big with [Shakey Graves and Dope Lemon ($54.50, kids 12 and under free)](https://community.sevendaysvt.com/event/ben-and-jerrys-concerts-on-the-green-shakey-graves-and-dope-lemon-45316805) at Ben & Jerry's Concerts on the Green at Shelburne Museum, music at 6, or a sunset [hip hop dance cruise aboard the Spirit of Ethan Allen](https://www.facebook.com/events/1287775823199372/) at 6:30 if you would rather groove on the water.

## Local News (All Links Clickable)

[Shipwreck Hunters Capture Stunning Images of Their Finds](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/arts-culture/history-arts-culture/local-shipwreck-hunters-capture-stunning-images-of-their-finds/)

❝

*"Of the more than 5,000 underwater objects that the Lefebvres have discovered and documented in the past 14 years, the Roadster is neither the oldest nor the most impressive, but Gary has an understandable attachment to it," per Seven Days.*

Gary and Ellen Lefebvre of Malletts Bay have spent 14 years scanning Lake Champlain from their research vessel, turning sonar hits into gorgeous 3D renderings of wrecks that even technical divers cannot reach, including the paddle wheels of the Phoenix, an 1819 steamboat wreck they located in 2020. They share the images publicly, help the Lake Champlain Maritime Museum monitor the lake's roughly 300 known wrecks, and have assisted the Coast Guard in recovering drowning victims. The Roadster that opens the story turned out to hold a family connection, since one of the two people who drowned with it in 1936 was Gary's great uncle. A remarkable local rabbit hole worth your full read.

[Former Courtyard by Marriott in Burlington becomes The Harborvale following renovation](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/burlingtons-courtyard-marriott-harborvale/73236248)

❝

*"A longtime Burlington hotel has a new name and a refreshed look following a $19 million renovation," per NBC5.*

The 161 room Cherry Street hotel stayed open through the phased makeover and now operates as The Harborvale, with Westport Hospitality, the same group behind Hotel Vermont, betting big on downtown. Managing director Hans Van Wees framed the investment as long term confidence in Burlington even while acknowledging that Canadian tourism dipped after the first tariff round and faces renewed uncertainty now. Worth watching how a full service independent brand fares on a block that counts on cross border visitors.

[The Festival of Fools celebrates 18 years in Burlington](https://www.charlottenewsvt.org/2026/07/23/the-festival-of-fools-celebrates-18-years-in-burlington/)

❝

*"These remarkable performers, along with several others, will spend the weekend transforming Church Street into one giant outdoor stage filled with laughter, wonder and surprise," per the Charlotte News.*

Festival cofounder Woody Keppel tells the origin story himself, an unplanned busking stop on Church Street decades ago that convinced him the bricks were built for street performance. This year's edition lands next weekend with the surreal physical theater act Galumpha, street dancer Snap Boogie, yo-yo artist John Higby, aerialist Cate Great, and a kickoff cabaret at the Venetian Soda Lounge on Thursday, July 30. Mark it now, because Church Street during Fools weekend is one of the best free afternoons of the Burlington summer.

[Concerns Mount Over Young Riders Operating E-Bikes and E-Motos](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/news-news/concerns-mount-over-young-riders-operating-e-bikes-and-e-motos/)

❝

*"We have this lawless behavior that's disregarding other users of bike paths, and also on the street and sidewalks," per Seven Days.*

Chittenden County towns are scrambling to catch up with kids as young as 9 riding e-bikes and far more powerful e-motos, which can hit 55 mph and are illegal for riders under 16. Burlington police have responded to at least six e-bike crashes this spring and summer, a UVM pediatric ICU doctor reports rising serious injuries, and South Burlington, Colchester, and Burlington have all added signs, school outreach, or ordinance discussions. Local Motion plans to lobby next year for penalties on souping up e-bikes and for truth in advertising rules, since some e-motos are sold as e-bikes.

[Friends remember 16-year-old killed in Burlington e-bike crash last week](https://www.wcax.com/2026/07/20/friends-remember-16-year-old-killed-burlington-e-bike-crash-last-week/)

[Burlington restaurants, breweries brace for impact of President Trump's newest wave of tariffs](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/burlington-restaurants-breweries-trump-tariffs/73223735)

❝

*"We'll have to pay more, and in turn we'll have to charge more," per NBC5.*

[Progressive Activist Challenges Incumbent Lawman for Chittenden County Sheriff](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/election-2026/progressive-activist-challenges-incumbent-lawman-for-chittenden-county-sheriff/)

❝

*"The race for the Democratic nomination comes at a time when Vermont sheriffs are under increased scrutiny amid several high-profile scandals," per Seven Days.*

Kevin Bloom, a 32 year old Burlington audio producer with no law enforcement experience, is running on eviction reform and a first of its kind jobs program that would connect tenants behind on rent with employers willing to help pay off the debt. [Vermont Gallium Nitride Tech Hub opens in South Burlington](https://www.wcax.com/2026/07/24/vermont-gallium-nitride-tech-hub-opens-south-burlington/)

❝

*"The lab allows Vermont and New England tech companies to experiment with gallium nitride, an emerging technology that can be used as a replacement for silicon in electronics chips," per WCAX.*

V-GAN is now open for business at OnLogic's South Burlington headquarters, giving regional companies access to prototyping and testing that is usually too expensive to pursue alone. The project survived a near death experience when federal funding was cut last year after being announced under the previous administration, and its future operations still are not fully funded. A quiet but meaningful bet on keeping chip innovation, and the jobs that follow it, anchored in Chittenden County.

[Is it safe to eat lettuce? Upper Valley experts answer key questions on cyclosporiasis outbreak](https://vtdigger.org/2026/07/22/is-it-safe-to-eat-lettuce-upper-valley-experts-answer-key-questions-on-cyclosporiasis-outbreak/)

❝

*"As of July 13, the CDC has confirmed 1,645 cases of cyclospora infection and 141 hospitalizations across 34 states," per VTDigger.*

The good news first, since Vermont has recorded no cases tied to the national outbreak and the recalled iceberg lettuce was not distributed here. The parasite rides in on imported produce, so the experts' advice is to wash everything well, seek care if a gastrointestinal bug drags on for weeks, and know that local farmstand and farmers market produce carries very low risk. Co-ops and markets in the region are actually seeing a bump in local produce sales as shoppers steer around the big brands, a silver lining for the farmers you will see at the markets this weekend.

[Revolutionary War cannon fragment pulled from Lake Champlain](https://www.wcax.com/2026/07/20/revolutionary-war-cannon-fragment-pulled-lake-champlain/)

❝

*"Once brought on shore, it was confirmed to be the last part of a cannon that belonged to the gunboat New York, one of eight under Benedict Arnold's command during the Battle of Valcour in 1776," per WCAX.*

Researchers off Valcour Island recovered the final missing piece of a cannon that exploded during one of America's earliest naval battles, killing Lt. Thomas Rogers, nearly 250 years ago. The fragment joins the rest of the gun at the Lake Champlain Maritime Museum, and for retired trooper turned underwater archaeologist Ed Scollon, who found the cannon almost 30 years ago, it completes the puzzle of a career. Between this and the Lefebvres up top, the lake gave up a lot of history this week.

[Vermont Brewers Festival tries to entice Canadian attendees as tourism remains low](https://vtcommunitynews.org/2026/07/23/vermont-brewers-festival-tries-to-entice-canadian-attendees-as-tourism-remains-low/)

❝

*"Before Canadians stopped visiting, we would sell 600-800 tickets to Canadians," per the Community News Service. "Last year, we sold 75 tickets (to Canadians)."*

The Brewers Association tried something clever for the 33rd festival at Waterfront Park in late July, selling tickets to Canadians at par, $60 Canadian instead of the $84.49 the exchange rate would demand. It worked, sort of, with about 190 Canadian tickets sold, more than double last year but a long way from the 600 plus of the before times. Organizers plan to run the at par offer again next year, and breweries near the border are flying Canadian flags and printing French menus to say what the festival said, that Canadians are still neighbors here.

[Burlington apartment fire displaces 9 residents](https://www.wcax.com/2026/07/23/burlington-apartment-fire-displaces-9-residents/)

[Scott Says Vermont Will Prioritize Local Media When Purchasing Advertisements](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/scott-says-vermont-will-prioritize-local-media-when-purchasing-advertisements/)

❝

*"I've always been a supporter of 'buying local' and that's just as important for local newspapers and stations as it is for our local hardware stores or farms," per Seven Days.*

Gov. Scott signed an executive order directing state departments to prefer local print, digital, radio, and TV outlets when buying ads, excluding the tourism campaigns aimed out of state. The stakes are real, since Vermont lost 53 percent of its journalists between 2013 and 2023, and the state spends roughly $8 million a year on advertising with about half exempt as tourism.
