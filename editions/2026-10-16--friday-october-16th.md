## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

Find the warm coat, because this weekend swings from soggy to wintry to gorgeous in three days. Friday is wet and windy, with rain heavy at times, a break around mid morning, then more showers along a cold front this afternoon and early evening, a high near 60 and south gusts around 40 mph in the Champlain Valley that could bring down a few branches. The showers end this evening as the wind turns northwest, and tonight drops to the mid 30s while the mountains turn to snow. Saturday is the coldest day of the season so far, with highs only around 40, northwest gusts near 30 mph and morning wind chills in the 20s, and a few stray flakes could even mix in down here in the morning before the sun comes out in the afternoon. Saturday night drops right to freezing, then Sunday turns sunny with a high in the mid 50s, and Monday is sunny and near 60 to start a long dry stretch. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

## Local News (All Links Clickable)

**[New plans could bring Burlington’s Nectar’s back to life](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/15/new-plans-could-bring-burlingtons-nectars-back-life/)**

❝

*"It’s very exciting news. You know, I’ve long thought that the closing of Nectar’s was one of the big reasons that this stretch of town was so barren for a while," per WCAX.*

The Main Street club has been closed since last year, and Shawn Cliche has submitted a zoning application to use all three floors for food, live music, DJs and pool tables, day and night. He says it's still undecided whether the Nectar's name stays, and there's a long process ahead before music returns. Cliche has opened about 30 bars and clubs over the years, including a similar venue in Plattsburgh.

**[Yên Vietnamese Café & Kitchen to Launch Soon in Winooski](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/foodnews/yen-vietnamese-cafe-kitchen-to-launch-soon-in-winooski)**

❝

*"It means the sense of peace and comfort that you feel on the inside," per Seven Days.*

Summer Cao, 27, moved Donwoori Korean restaurant to 22 Main Street by the Winooski rotary and kept the lease on its old home at 65 Winooski Falls Way, where she expects to open Yên by the end of October. The 35 seat restaurant honors her late father with pho, fresh summer rolls, sticky fish sauce chicken wings and Vietnamese coffee made with robusta beans, plus mocktails and cocktails, for lunch and dinner six days a week.

**[Burlington mayor signs executive order on ICE enforcement; DHS pushes back](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/burlington-mayor-ice-enforcement-order-dhs/74137301)**

❝

*"This is nothing more than a political stunt by Vermont sanctuary politicians," per NBC5.*

Mayor Emma Mulvaney-Stanak's order, signed Wednesday, lays out how Burlington will carry out Act 150, the state law in effect since June that restricts civil arrests, including immigration arrests, at schools, churches, libraries, hospitals, polling places and other designated locations. The city will update its signs, train employees on responding to federal immigration enforcement and work with legal organizations to help residents understand their rights, and Burlington police will not assist with civil immigration enforcement. DHS says ICE isn't targeting hospitals, schools or churches and isn't planning operations at polling places.

**[ICE is more active in Vermont. But federal data overstates it](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-13/ice-is-more-active-in-vermont-but-federal-data-overstates-it)**

❝

*"So ICE does appear to be on track to increase its interior arrests in Vermont this year, even if not as dramatically as the federal data suggests," per Vermont Public.*

Widely cited federal data shows ICE logging 124 arrests in Vermont through July, more than triple all of 2025, but Vermont Public found more than 50 of those people closely match border crossers caught by Border Patrol and later handed to ICE, so their arrests were counted twice. ICE made 29 at large arrests in that stretch, including the March 11 detentions of three immigrants at their South Burlington home, compared with 35 in all of 2025. Since that data was compiled, Seven Days has identified at least 12 construction workers detained by ICE in August and early September.

**[‘Fuck the Flock cameras’: On big tech, Vermont’s candidates for governor differ on style and substance](https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/14/fuck-the-flock-cameras-on-big-tech-vermonts-candidates-for-governor-differ-on-style-and-substance)**

❝

*"I think what you’re seeing here is an issue that really gets to the heart of the different governing philosophies between the two," per VTDigger.*

Democratic nominee Amanda Janoo wants to ban Flock Safety's license plate reading cameras, which some Vermont police departments use, and to tell large data center companies they aren't welcome here for now, after Gov. Phil Scott vetoed a bill this year that would have regulated where they could be built. Scott says the federal government, working with the private sector, should take the lead on regulating AI. His campaign says he would support a moratorium on large data center construction while the state watches what Washington does, and Janoo backs one too, along with new taxes on digital advertising revenue and what her campaign calls the nation's strongest public standards for AI. Polls and campaign spending suggest this could be the closest governor's race in a decade.

**[Pro-Phil Scott super PAC launches ad blitz targeting Amanda Janoo](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-12/pro-phil-scott-super-pac-launches-ad-blitz-targeting-amanda-janoo)**

❝

*"National Republicans don’t spend almost half a million dollars to help keep a governor in office without a reason," per Vermont Public.*

A Stronger Vermont, the Republican Governors Association's super PAC that spent $3 million to help elect Scott in 2016, spent nearly $175,000 late last week on TV and online ads painting Janoo as too far left, using a "magical thinking" line from her primary opponent Aly Richards, who has since criticized the RGA for misappropriating her quotes. Scott's campaign says the ads make fair points and that the RGA's help won't change his approach to the Trump administration. The latest Vermont Public poll put Scott's approval at 45%, down 15 points from 2022, and Janoo has outraised him $706,000 to $414,000.

**[Kismayo Kitchen Reopens in BTV on Riverside Ave — for Now](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/foodnews/kismayo-kitchen-reopens-in-btv-on-riverside-ave-for-now)**

❝

*"Mohamed and Abdirisak both emphasized that they are actively seeking a new Chittenden County home for Kismayo Kitchen," per Seven Days.*

The Somali American restaurant founded by the late Ahmed Omar reopened the weekend of Oct. 10 at 505 Riverside Ave., now run by his widow, Anisa Mohamed, with siblings Maryan and Abdirisak Maalin, keeping the mix of samosas, goat curry and Philly cheesesteaks with a slight price increase planned. The building qualified for a FEMA buyout and will eventually be torn down for green space, most likely in 2027, so they're hunting for a spot with at least 24 seats.
