## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

Keep an extra layer near the costume this weekend, because Halloween looks dry, sunny and chilly. Friday stays cooler and breezy with more clouds than sun, a high in the mid 50s and gusts to about 25 from midday through tonight, and while a stray shower is possible, most places stay dry. Saturday is sunny and tops out around 50, and trick or treat runs from the upper 40s at 4 to about 40 by 9 with clear skies and barely any wind. Clocks fall back an hour Saturday night, and Sunday is the nicest day of the weekend, sunny and about 57 with the evening in the upper 40s. Monday reaches about 60 with more clouds and stays dry through the day. [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html) keeps the forecast going all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores.

**Friday**

[Dedication of 'Jambo'](https://frontporchforum.com/calendar/events/525602) **– 5 PM:** South Burlington dedicates Jambo, an original David Stromeyer artwork and a new permanent addition to the city's public art collection, at Veterans Memorial Park near the entrance at Dorset and Swift streets. Expect remarks and light refreshments, with the piece made possible by a donation from community member Todd Lockwood.

[Community Night: Frank & Stein](https://www.helloburlingtonvt.com/event/community-night%3a-frank-%26-stein/8055/) **– 5 PM:** August First on South Champlain Street opens after dark until 8 for a free Halloween kickoff in support of Migrant Justice, with a hot dog menu from Chef Mike P., autumnal drinks and fall pastries. Frankenweenie plays on the wall, costumes are encouraged and families are welcome.

[Adventure Dinner Monsters Ball](https://www.adventuredinner.com/shop/p/oct-29-30-monsters-ball-adventure-dinner) **– 6 PM:** Chef Eric Hodet serves five courses themed to classic monsters at the Adventure Dinner Clubhouse in Colchester, including Frankenstein's Monster as a pork belly and beef roulade and Count Dracula as dark chocolate mousse with red wine cherries ($75 plus tax). The night opens with a Haunting Hour in the lounge and den, with cocktails and mocktails for purchase at the Spooky Bar, and includes a Best Dressed Costume Contest.

[Galloween](https://www-vermontcomedyclub-com.seatengine.com/shows/389711) **– 6:30 PM:** Natalie Miller hosts "a spooky night of non-dude comedy" at Vermont Comedy Club, with a lineup of local standup, improv, sketch and musical comedians. Dudes are welcome, though the page warns that anyone who harshes the vibe gets locked in the basement ($15, $10 students).

[Queen City Ghostwalk: Darkness Falls](https://www.sevendaystickets.com/e/darkness-falls-tour-2) **– 7 PM:** Thea Lewis's downtown ghost tour meets at the Democracy Sculpture on Courthouse Plaza for an hour of Burlington's infamous characters and haunts. Tonight is Glow Stick Night and Saturday is Halloween, the last two walks of the season ($25).

[Monster Bash 2026](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/monster-bash-2026-odyssey-after-dark-sips-sirens-and-scares-tickets-2001402686311) **– 7 PM:** The Old Lantern's 16th annual costume party in Charlotte goes with an "Odyssey After Dark" theme, with casino tables, dancing, raffle prizes and a food truck menu, and it's 21+ ($69.82 and up). [Farewell to GNOP](https://www.girlsniteoutvt.com/farewell) **– 7:30 PM:** Girls Nite Out Productions closes the curtain after 15 years with an evening of special performances, live music and memories at the Black Box Theater at Main Street Landing, plus a guest appearance by WJOY's Ginny McGehee. ## Local News (All Links Clickable)

[Charlie’s Boathouse to turn into public park after family sells to Winooski Valley Park District](https://www.colchestersun.com/things_to_do/charlie-s-boathouse-to-turn-into-public-park-after-family-sells-to-winooski-valley-park/article_25d09468-2f5a-4ecc-acc0-e4c001dbeaf0.html)

❝

*"It's literally at the river mouth, so it's highly unique, and it is kind of a puzzle piece," Warner said, per the Colchester Sun.*

The boat rental and snack bar spot on the Burlington bike path by the Winooski River bridge, which ran until Charlie died in 2021, is becoming a public park after his family sold the structure and land to the Winooski Valley Park District. Director Nick Warner says the site has 600 feet of shoreline and a conservation easement, so the plan leans toward non motorized recreation, with visitors arriving by the bike path and a new ramp as the first phase. The public can weigh in on the site and the historic building through WVPD's website and hearings in the coming weeks, and it's likely to open sometime in the spring.

[Perkins Museum of Geology reopens at UVM after six-year closure](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/perkins-museum-of-geology-reopens/74277873)

❝

*"And it's also very entertaining. People, when they come here, are clearly struck by the awesomeness of some of the exhibits," per NBC5.*

Closed since 2020, first for COVID and then for staffing shortages, UVM's geology museum is open again with the Charlotte Whale, Vermont's official state fossil, among its displays and student interns helping develop and present the exhibits. It's free and open Monday through Friday from 9 to 4, with a new exhibit on the global diversity of the collection planned for spring.

[Phil Scott built a broad coalition. Now it's showing cracks](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-28/phil-scott-built-broad-coalition-now-showing-cracks)

❝

*"I think in this race we see a wave, a blue wave, coming across the country that is even affecting Vermont," he said, per Vermont Public.*

Gov. Phil Scott won 71% of the vote two years ago, and Vermont Public reports that coalition is fracturing as he asks for a sixth term that would make him Vermont's longest serving governor. The [latest poll](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/27/new-poll-shows-vermont-governors-race-near-dead-heat) from State Navigate has Scott at 49 and Democratic/Progressive challenger Amanda Janoo at 46, with a [margin of error of 2.3 points](https://projects.statenavigate.com/interactives/vt/statewide-poll-october-23-2026/vermont-statewide-poll-october-23-25-2026.pdf). At [Wednesday's WCAX debate](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/29/scott-janoo-outline-competing-economic-views-wcax-debate), Janoo pressed her Fair Share proposal to tax income over $250,000 and help stand up universal primary care, while conceding she doesn't know how the budget gets to a full universal system, which she called the "ultimate destination." Scott answered that "the math has to add up." Scott's signature issue remains his opposition to new taxes, and the Republican Governors Association has spent more than $930,000 through a pro Scott super PAC on ads attacking Janoo. Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3.

[Burlington Mayor Taps Anthony Geraci as City’s Next Police Chief](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-mayor-taps-anthony-geraci-as-citys-next-police-chief)

❝

*"I am very confident in advancing his appointment as our next chief of police," she said, per Seven Days.*

Mayor Emma Mulvaney-Stanak's pick began with the NYPD in 1998, spent about 15 years with Albany police, and later ran the Watervliet and Newburgh departments in New York. He left Newburgh in 2024 after three women accused him of gender discrimination, retaliation and creating a hostile work environment. He was cleared of any official wrongdoing and called the claims a smear campaign, and the mayor says her administration dug into the complaint and she checked his references herself. The council will be asked to approve him on Nov. 9, with the department down to 55 officers as of August from 92 in July 2020.
