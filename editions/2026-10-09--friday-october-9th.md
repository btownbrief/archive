## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

If you have outdoor plans this weekend, Saturday is the day to make them. Friday brings a mix of sun and clouds with a high near 59 and a gusty west wind, plus a round of pop up showers late this afternoon into the early evening. Tonight drops to around 40, and frost is likely away from the lake by Saturday morning, so cover or bring in anything tender. Saturday is sunny all day with light winds and a high near 57. Sunday turns milder, near 65 on a breezy south wind, as clouds thicken ahead of the remnants of Isaias, which are likely to bring showers Sunday night, mostly before 2 AM. Monday dries out after a possible stray shower early, with a bit of sun and a high near 66, before more rain arrives Tuesday. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

Friday starts on College Street, where a Burlington native opens [Shakurs Vintage](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeNTSAjqwmh/), a Y2K and vintage shop on the second floor of 209 College that also carries pieces from a few Burlington Vintage Market regulars. Runners can meet at Burlington Beer Co. at 5:50 for the Green Mountain Athletic Association's monthly [beer and social run](https://www.instagram.com/gmaavt/), with a route preview before heading out at 6 and two route options for shorter and longer distances.

## Local News (All Links Clickable)

[Burlington radio station celebrates 80 years on air](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/wjoy-celebrates-80-years-on-air/74084048)

❝

*"I've got the best listeners. I'm sort of on the AM dial, which is rare these days, to not be talk or religion or other formats, but the one thing I have is a lot of different age groups," per NBC5.*

WJOY began broadcasting in 1946 and moved to its current home on Joy Drive in South Burlington in 1967. On Thursday the station marked 80 years by inviting listeners in to reminisce, and host Ginny McGehee was there to celebrate with them. Happy 80th, WJOY!

[One Dish: Sabah’s House Shares Shawarma From a Family Recipe](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/food-drink/restaurantreviews/one-dish-sabahs-house-shares-shawarma-from-a-family-recipe)

❝

*"Sometimes nobody orders anything but shawarma," per Seven Days.*

Mustafa Khudaier, 19, reopened the city owned kiosk at the corner of Church and College streets in late June as Sabah's House, the spot Leunig's used to run as Petit Bijou, and his mother, Sabah Abbas, is the one with the recipes. The family came to Vermont 12 years ago as refugees from the Iraq War, and the $14 chicken shawarma wrap has already built a loyal following. Sabah's House opens at 10 every day except Monday, though made to order dishes like the shawarma don't start until noon.

[Governor proposes funding Burlington shelter with overdose center money](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/07/governor-proposes-funding-burlington-shelter-with-overdose-center-money)

❝

*"I would take the money that’s been appropriated to that and put it toward the emergency shelter and work toward that if that’s the higher priority," per WCAX.*

Speaking in Rutland on Wednesday, Gov. Phil Scott suggested moving the money set aside for Burlington's proposed overdose prevention center toward the low barrier, 100 bed shelter for chronically homeless people that city and business leaders keep calling for. He still believes a congregate shelter is the wrong approach but says he won't oppose it if the city has a location and people to staff it, and his administration is also looking at a handful of smaller shelters across Chittenden County.

[Hurdles Remain for Burlington’s Overdose-Prevention Center](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/opioids/hurdles-remain-for-burlingtons-overdose-prevention-center)

❝

*"We are not in the business of forcing this on any particular neighborhood," per Seven Days.*

More than two years after a 2024 law cleared the way for it, the center still hasn't opened, and the city has signed a letter of intent on a building while declining to say even which neighborhood it's in. There's no zoning classification for the use yet, so the planning commission has to write rules, and a bloc of council Democrats wants it kept out of the downtown core and waterfront or doubts it's needed at all. Operator Vermonters for Criminal Justice Reform has looked at about a dozen properties in 18 months, and the center would cost an estimated $1 million a year from opioid settlement money. Overdose deaths fell 25% statewide in 2025, but Chittenden County recorded 18 in the first six months of this year, well above its three year average of 11.

[Police face barrage of questions but provide few answers about shooting by Williston officer](https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/08/police-face-barrage-of-questions-but-provide-few-answers-about-shooting-by-williston-officer)

❝

*"A vehicle approached officers, and we’re not exactly sure yet what occurred during that," per VTDigger.*

Around 4:50 Thursday morning, a man drove up to Williston officers who were dealing with downed wires across Route 2A, and one of them shot him in the hip. The man has since been released from the hospital, the road reopened around 4 that afternoon, and two officers are on paid administrative leave while state police investigate. At Thursday's press conference, police said they still had to review body and cruiser camera footage and would not identify anyone involved.

['Tailgate party for birders' will commence in Huntington this weekend](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-08/tailgate-party-for-birders-will-commence-in-huntington-this-weekend)

❝

*"It’s a very relaxed birding event because we sit around and we bird, but we also talk and tell jokes and drink coffee," per Vermont Public.*

The Birds of Vermont Museum in Huntington hosts its 12 hour Big Sit! on Sunday from 6 AM to 6 PM, with birders camped in a 17 foot circle counting every species they spot. The international event started in New Haven, Connecticut, in 1992, the museum has hosted its own circle since 2003, and donations are pledged based on how many species turn up. No birding experience is required, and you can show up any time during the 12 hours.

[Parents of cyclist who was killed in crosswalk file wrongful death lawsuit against driver](https://vtdigger.org/2026/10/07/parents-of-cyclist-who-was-killed-in-crosswalk-file-wrongful-death-lawsuit-against-driver)

❝

*"We cannot change what happened, but we can insist on accountability," per VTDigger.*

Shiloh Skalka, 16, a Burlington High School student, was killed on her e-bike in the crosswalk at Pine and Locust streets on July 16, and her parents are now suing driver Douglas Knapp, the 80 year old folk artist known as dug Nap, for unspecified damages. The suit says the crossing's warning lights had been flashing for 7 seconds when the crash happened, and police estimated he was driving 28 mph in a 25 zone. Knapp has pleaded not guilty to a misdemeanor charge of negligent operation of a motor vehicle resulting in her death.

[Beware of were-hens: Vermont theater company launches successful Halloween show](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/shelburne-players-hatchford-hotel/74081418)

❝

*"We like animals, stories about animals and paranormal things. There are were-hens throughout the show and lots of other weird creatures," per NBC5.*

Hatchford Hotel is an original Halloween musical comedy written by Greg Skillman and Adam Rabin, and it's only the second musical the Shelburne Players have staged in their 24 years. The show heads into its second weekend with this weekend's performances almost sold out, though there is a waitlist.

[Valcour Island’s Unknown Soldiers Are Finally Laid to Rest](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/arts-culture/history-arts-culture/valcour-islands-unknown-soldiers-are-finally-laid-to-rest)

❝

*"The soldier’s teeth sat on a shelf for 159 years," per Seven Days.*

The gunboat Philadelphia sank at the Battle of Valcour Island in 1776 and stayed on the bottom of Lake Champlain until diver Lorenzo Hagglund found the wreck in 1935, and teeth recovered with it will now be buried with full military honors. The funeral, dedicated to five soldiers believed to have died in the battle, is Tuesday, Oct. 13, at Saratoga National Cemetery in Schuylerville, N.Y. The Philadelphia itself, the country's oldest surviving warship, is undergoing a $1.7 million cleaning and conservation effort.

[Festivities underway for 250th anniversary of the Battle of Valcour](https://www.mynbc5.com/article/festivities-battle-of-valcour/74082632)

❝

*"What our goal is is to let people know about the story of the Battle of Valcour Island and Benedict Arnold, who was a hero at that time, and the impact it had on the shaping of this nation," per NBC5.*

Sunday marks 250 years since the battle, and Plattsburgh's one time weekend of events runs Friday through Sunday. The highlight is Sunday's reenactment with replica boats on Lake Champlain, staged off Plattsburgh City Beach so it's easier to watch, with more than 100 reenactors from as far as Ohio and Virginia, and a memorial service closes out the weekend.

[Fossil fuel prices are up, and Vermonters are struggling to foot the bill](https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-10-08/fossil-fuel-prices-vermonters-struggling-foot-bill)

❝

*"This isn’t something you should be ashamed of. It is something that is there to help you get through the winter," per Vermont Public.*

Heating oil is up about $2 a gallon from this time last year and gasoline almost $1.50, and Northeast Kingdom Community Action says it's getting upwards of 20 calls a day, including from households earning $60,000 to $70,000. State officials expect this year's federal LIHEAP heating assistance to be released on time, before Nov. 1, and federal assistance is generally available to households at 185% of the federal poverty level or less, about $2,500 a month for a household of one. Crisis fuel assistance is available up to 200%.
