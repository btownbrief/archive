## Weather & Weekend Rundown:

Labor Day weekend arrives with the friendliest forecast we have had in a while, so go ahead and make plans. Friday is the warm one, mostly sunny and near 78, with a stray shower possible between 1 and 3 in the afternoon and a cooler night in the mid 50s behind it. Saturday runs partly sunny and breezier near 73, and the weekend's only real rain chance is a passing shower or storm around mid afternoon, so keep the fair and market plans a little loose for an hour or two. Sunday clears out completely, mostly sunny near 73 with the humidity gone, and Labor Day Monday is the prize, sunny and near 78. Track it all week, with patio, swim, and sunset scores, at [Burlington Right Now](https://guide.btownbrief.com/weather.html).

Friday belongs to the fair and the South End. The [Champlain Valley Fair](https://champlainvalleyfair.org/faqs/) runs noon to 10 in Essex Junction today and adult admission drops to $10 after 5, then [ZZ Top with George Thorogood & the Destroyers ($39-69)](https://champlainvalleyfair.org/event/zz-top/champlain-valley-fair/essex-junction-vermont/) take the grandstand at 6 with doors at 5. Down on Pine Street, the organizers say tonight is [the last South End Get Down of the summer](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc1wQsoJtM_/), so swing through [the block party](https://www.getdownvt.com/) between 5 and 9 for one more lap of the food trucks and a goodbye to your favorite vendors. Over in South Burlington, the [South Burlington Farmers Market](https://www.southburlingtonvt.gov/702/South-Burlington-Farmers-Market) sets up on the airport rec path from 5 to 7 with Bart Feller playing, and the first 75 households through get $5 in market spending money. The [Waterfront Silent Disco ($25)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcuTGG9tCXD/) meets at 6 at 1 College Street in front of ECHO, headphones on, dancing along the water. Music people have a free night if they want one. New York cosmic soul act [Lady Moon & The Eclipse](https://www.foambrewers.com/events/lady-moon-the-eclipse-w-beccs) plays Foam at 9 with Beccs opening, free and all ages, and [Alligator Wine](https://skinnypancake.com/event/dancing-in-the-streets-alligator-wine/) plays a free Dancing in the Streets set on the Skinny Pancake waterfront patio from 8 to 11, while 90 Proof plays [the Spanked Puppy](https://www.facebook.com/events/the-spanked-puppy/90-proof-the-spanked-puppy/37779810881618026/) in Colchester Friday evening. [Punk Rock Queer Prom ($10)](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/radiobean/2338308) fills the Light Club Lamp Shop at 8 with four bands, Cat Crash, the Eyetraps, Emo Girls Kissing and We're Here! To Kill, 21 plus. Comedy fans, Matthew Broussard's 7 PM shows at [Vermont Comedy Club ($30)](https://www-vermontcomedyclub-com.seatengine.com/events/125898) are sold out both nights, but the 9 PM shows Friday and Saturday still have seats. And a heads up for anyone hoping to catch [Sierra Ferrell](https://highergroundmusic.com/events/sierra-ferrell/) on the green at Shelburne Museum tonight, the show is sold out and Higher Ground's waitlist and ticket exchange are the only ways in.

Saturday is the fullest day of the weekend, and we are out in it. Our own [BTown Coffee Club [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/316140333/) takes the big outdoor table Zero Gravity at 10, no agenda, just coffee and good conversation, and then a bunch of us are heading to the fair. [Btown at the Champlain Valley Fair [MEETUP]](https://www.meetup.com/burlington-social-activites-group/events/316166956/) meets at 1 at Expo North, the first big building after you come in through the Red Gate by the parking lot. Weekend admission is $20, an unlimited ride bracelet is $40 and parking is $10 a car, so carpool if you can, and come eat fried things with us. Pine Street does its market double header, the [Burlington Farmers Market](http://www.burlingtonfarmersmarket.org) from 9 to 2 at 345 Pine, and the [Burlington Vintage Market Fall Flea](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcweNgNK9a0/) right next door at Barge Canal, also 9 to 2, with 15 plus dealers of vintage clothing, vinyl and $5 piles. **[The Homegrown in Vermont Music Festival Returns](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/music/musicnews/the-homegrown-in-vermont-music-festival-returns)**

❝

*"So when we get to the fest, it's almost like a Vermont singer-songwriter summer camp," per Seven Days.*

The third Homegrown in Vermont festival takes over Spruce Peak Arts in Stowe on Saturday with more than 20 acts alternating across two stages so the music never stops, including Madaila, Ali McGuirk, Kyle Chadburn, Reid Parsons and organizer Troy Millette. The closer is Guster front man and Williston resident Ryan Miller, backed by Hadestown composer Michael Chorney's seven piece Freeway Clyde, playing Tom Petty's Wildflowers front to back. Last year Miller led a Vermont all star Kinks tribute at the same fest, which draws more than 1,000 people.

**[Local boat captain discovers thousands of tires at bottom of Lake Champlain](https://www.wcax.com/2026/09/01/local-boat-captain-discovers-thousands-tires-bottom-lake-champlain)**

❝

*"These tires were to break loose and end up on a public beach or break loose and end up floating and hitting a boat at high speed -- that's the main concern with it," per WCAX.*

Boat captain Gary Lefebvre's sonar picked up thousands of tires strung together along the bottom of Cumberland Bay between Crab Island and the Plattsburgh Boat Basin in early August, a mass he estimates at 100 to 200 yards long that looks like an old breakwater lost in transit. A month later they are still down there, and New York's Department of Environmental Conservation says it is looking into whether to open an investigation.

**[Burlington's Radio Bean to Close Next Week](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/music/musicnews/burlingtons-radio-bean-to-close-next-week)**

❝

*"Currently, the Bean is facing a very serious, immediate, and seemingly insurmountable financial obstacle," per Seven Days.*

Owner Lee Anderson announced Wednesday that a $29,000 liquor liability bill from the club's insurer, due immediately, forced the decision, and the Bean's last official day is Wednesday, September 9, with the Light Club Lamp Shop next door closing too. Anderson opened Radio Bean in 2000 and said in January he would hand it to a new ownership group this summer, a transition the finances have now stalled, though he says the closure could last weeks or months rather than forever. He floated a mini festival at the club over Labor Day weekend to end with a bang, so watch their socials.

**[Vermont ends night and weekend intake for state-funded motel rooms](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/01/vermont-ends-night-and-weekend-intake-for-state-funded-motel-rooms)**

❝

*"It is no longer an emergency housing program," per VTDigger.*

As of September 1, anyone seeking a state motel voucher has to call the state between 8 and 4:30 on weekdays, since the night and weekend intake that Vermont 211 handled has ended, and in July alone 211 took 1,024 housing calls tied to the program and did 541 intakes. The change is the first piece of the Vermont Homeless Response Continuum the Legislature passed in June, which also caps stays at 70 days outside the winter months, lowers the room caps and pairs each recipient with a case manager. Advocates like End Homelessness Vermont's Brenda Siegel say the law was meant to make access more flexible and call this the most restrictive version they have seen, so the tradeoff is better services for fewer people.

**[Burlington Recommends Prosecutor's Dog Be Put Down](https://www.sevendaysvt.com/news/burlington-recommends-prosecutors-dog-be-put-down)**

❝

*"This is a problem that should have been solved before I went to the hospital with my upper lip hanging from my face," per Seven Days.*

The city delivered its recommendation Monday night to the Animal Control Committee, which heard from several people who said Moose bit or charged them over three years and will issue a written decision in the coming weeks. Owner Diane Wheeler, a Franklin County deputy prosecutor who plans to retire next month, appeared by video and argued the committee has no jurisdiction because she and the dog have left Burlington, while admitting she let Moose off leash at times and drove him to her mother's New North End home in violation of earlier orders.

**['We're not going to have a business': Vermont companies say Trump's renewed trade war with Canada has them scrambling, again](https://vtdigger.org/2026/09/01/were-not-going-to-have-a-business-vermont-companies-say-trumps-renewed-trade-war-with-canada-has-them-scrambling-again)**

❝

*"If these tariffs stay active, we're not going to have a business," per VTDigger.*

Sen. Peter Welch gathered business owners at Jay Peak on Tuesday, where the resort's president said Canadian visitors dropped 35% last ski season and have gone from disappointed to angry, and a Newport snow groomer dealer said Canada's planned retaliatory tariffs would add about CA$250,000 to each of the three machines he is delivering across the border this month. Jasper Hill Farm had its worst year ever in 2025, lost a Canadian holiday order after the newest tariffs were announced and has paid duties as high as 50% on cheesemaking equipment for its Hardwick project. Welch has a bill with Sen. Kirsten Gillibrand to repeal the 1930 law behind the tariffs and refund what has been paid, though he admits it has little chance in this Congress.

**[UVM reports 2nd sighting of man peering into residence halls](https://www.wcax.com/2026/09/03/uvm-reports-2nd-sighting-man-peering-into-residence-halls)**

❝

*"Whether that was, you know, casing for theft or looking in windows for other reasons, we obviously don't know at this point, but suspicious enough that we're interested in identifying and chatting with him if possible," per WCAX.*

UVM released a new image Thursday after a second sighting Wednesday around 10 PM near Wing-Davis-Wilks on the Redstone campus, one night after the first, of a man believed to be 40 to 60 years old in a red shirt looking into residence hall windows. UVM Police could not find him and say there is no sign he entered a building. Anyone who spots him can call UVM Police at 802-656-3473 or use the CATSafe app.

**[UVM Medical Center quietly changed its policy for patient observation. Some staff are worried about patient safety.](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/31/uvm-medical-center-quietly-changed-its-policy-for-patient-observation-some-staff-are-worried-about-patient-safety)**

❝

*"There have been patients that I personally thought, 'I don't feel safe having this patient in my assignment without a CPSA with them,'" per VTDigger.*

In June the hospital quietly limited licensed nursing assistants from serving as sitters for patients at risk of falling, wandering or hurting themselves, steering those patients toward remote video monitoring or a pool of 17 patient safety attendants per shift covering 14 inpatient units and the emergency department. LNAs told VTDigger the remote monitors fail for patients with dementia or psychosis, one counted five incidents where safety was compromised, and two unions sent a cease and desist letter August 20 asking the hospital to pause the change while contracts are bargained. The backdrop is money, since UVM Medical Center projects a $43 million loss in the fiscal year that starts October 1 and the network has already proposed $140 million in cuts.

**[Vermont's Tibetan, Nepalese communities grieve after flooding](https://www.wcax.com/2026/08/29/vermonts-tibetan-nepalese-communities-grieve-after-flooding)**

❝

*"So many hundreds and hundreds of Tibetans, Nepalese, and even the foreigners, they lost their life, and we are here in solidarity for them," per WCAX.*

A glacier collapse high in the Himalayas set off a flash flood along the Nepal Tibet border on August 26, and Vermonters with family there are still struggling to reach them, including several employees of Namaste Kitchen Express in Burlington and Shelburne, whose owner Tuka Pradhan is raising money to send home. The Tibetan Association of Vermont held a prayer session for those lost and is collecting donations for relief in Nepal and Tibet. Interestingly enough, my direct coworker at GlobalFoundries is visiting family in Nepal for the past 2 weeks and is supposed to be back mid-September. Haven’t heard back from him yet; prayers him, his family, and friends are doing okay!

**[Ben & Jerry's legacy hits bumpy patch with latest turn in corporate saga](https://vtdigger.org/2026/08/28/ben-jerrys-legacy-hits-bumpy-patch-with-latest-turn-in-corporate-saga)**

❝

*"This is about the heart and soul of Ben & Jerry's," per VTDigger.*

Ben & Jerry's named three new directors to the independent board that guards its social mission on August 26 and signaled it intends to replace the Ben & Jerry's Foundation entirely, days after a judge tossed part of a lawsuit by former board members against parent The Magnum Ice Cream Company. Co-founder Ben Cohen says the appointments were made improperly and the fight is over who controls the brand's activism, while the company calls it governance under the merger agreement and points to an unreleased 2025 audit alleging mismanagement at the foundation, which Cohen denies. The foundation, which hands out roughly $600,000 a year in Vermont grants, was forced out of its South Burlington office last month, lost its company funding last year and expects its reserves to run dry by year's end. A new Ben & Jerry's Linked Prosperity Foundation was registered in January as an LLC by a former company finance head and a company lawyer, and no one from the current foundation has been offered a seat.

## UVM Athletics: No. 11 Men's Soccer Stays Unbeaten as the Catamounts Hit the Road

No. 11 Vermont closed out a four match homestand by [shutting out Holy Cross 2-0](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/9/1/mens-soccer-11-mens-soccer-secures-2-0-shutout-win-over-holy-cross.aspx) on Tuesday's Pep Rally night at Virtue Field, with Noel Bjork scoring his first goal as a Catamount in the seventh minute and Pieter Bultman adding his second in as many matches after halftime while the Cats held a 4-0 edge in shots on goal. That leaves them 3-0-1 and unbeaten through four for the second straight season, and they take their first road trip of the year Saturday to Sacred Heart at 5 on ESPN+.

Women's soccer had a tougher Thursday, [falling 5-1 to Boston College](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/9/3/womens-soccer-drop-non-conference-test-against-boston-college.aspx) on Youth Day and Poster Day at Virtue Field, though Eleni Stowell's 26th minute free kick was her first career goal and Sophia Kling made 5 saves. The Catamounts sit at 0-4-2 and visit Saint Joseph's on Sunday at 1.

Basketball season is taking shape too. The men [announced a non conference schedule](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/9/2/mens-basketball-announces-2026-27-non-conference-schedule.aspx) with a home opener against Quinnipiac on November 8 and a December 6 trip to Florida, the first meeting between the programs since 2015-16, and the women's [non conference slate](https://uvmathletics.com/news/2026/9/1/womens-basketball-womens-basketball-announces-2026-27-non-conference-slate.aspx) brings Oregon to Patrick Gym on November 11, a first for the program, after a home opener against Keene State on November 2.
